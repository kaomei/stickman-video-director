# B1 production and acceptance supplement

Required companion to [B1 · 极简手绘风](b1-minimal-handdrawn.md). First apply the [B common production and acceptance checklist](autopilot-production-qa.md) before planning, sample review and delivery. All B styles share its opening-30s hook, meaning-led staging, 2.5-second principal-picture progress, evidence/icons, natural caption phrasing and whole-film quality gates. This supplement adds **B1-only visual/audio defaults and accepted examples**; A and other B styles do not inherit those defaults or assets. Explicit user choices may customize B1 settings without silently dropping the B common quality floor.

Also apply the mandatory [B1/B2/B3 reference-effect acceptance protocol](b1-b2-b3-reference-acceptance.md): actually play/listen to Ryan V4, compare the encoded sample and full film, and record independent review separately from user approval. Complete production environments must target comparable effects; real tool limitations require observed evidence and an explicitly limited result rather than promoting the fixture or an encode pass to accepted quality. Equivalent language-specific design is required, not the identical English font.

## B1 acting and visual hierarchy

- Preserve the Ryan V4 slender proportions, fine slightly irregular outlines, near-white faint-grid paper and clearly exaggerated but varied expressions. Keep identity stable while adapting gaze, head/body angle, stance, hands and props to the script. A choice, effort, surprise and satisfaction need visibly different whole-body acting, not the same worried face or constant screaming.
- Author the story-specific poses and expressions needed; the engineering three-pose atlas is not a reusable presenter for every production. Make hands genuinely operate/touch supported objects, plant feet and show clear weight. Reuse valid cast/props for continuity rather than needless regeneration.
- Keep the promotional character-presence target around 60–70% of screen time, not picture area and not a quota for every genre. Permit character-free evidence, typography and object closeups; do not force a token presenter into each frame.
- Use recognizable hand-drawn objects, relevant entity marks/person sketches and coherent licensed icons with compatible line weight/roughness. Keep one dominant idea/action, negative space and useful detail instead of logo piles or decorative dashboards.
- Short, large hand-lettered scene text carries emphasis through selective weight, marker color or underlining. Captions remain the quieter access layer. Keep the paper/grid and hierarchy consistent outside clearly bounded real-media inserts.

## Apply the common hook, evidence and motion gates in B1

Plan the shared shot ledger against actual narration; the B1 version should name whole-body expression, prop contact, short scene text and hand-drawn visual payoff. Give the first 30 seconds a dedicated picture/SFX/copy hook pass, suited to the subject rather than a compulsory marketing script. The hook's promise must connect to the body/end. Do not copy the Full V4 script, channel metrics, timestamps or scene order.

For the shared 2.5-second rule, prefer meaningful hand/head/object motion, developing hand-drawn diagrams or relevant real moving media. Keep clear settling/reading time; no mandatory hard cuts, whole-frame drift as a substitute or repeat title → icon → underline builds. Compare semantic reveals to the [approved portrait](../assets/b1/portrait-approved-2026-11-13.mp4): the viewer should see the relevant elements develop in meaning order, followed by a readable result. Reject a revision that flattens the intended build into an already-complete opening frame, even when it preserves the final layout. Record observed reveal/action/result times for the actual story, not a fixed asset count or universal 1→2→3 recipe. Check distant layouts as well as adjacent scenes.

Apply the common sharpness, language, source and icon checks. B1 evidence inserts may use restrained rounded frames, a fine gray edge, light shadow and small accent. Keep borders complete and clear of captions/faces/hands, subordinate to the hand-drawn world. Check the final encoded crop, not merely the source resolution. Never present an illustrated/translated interface as genuine software footage.

## B1 captions and sound

Use one semantic caption line: landscape retains Noto Sans 600, **34 design units** (1280×720 baseline y=660); portrait uses **64 design units** on the 1280-wide design plane and inherits the hand-font slot (`font`, configured `AUTOPILOT_FONT`, then bundled Caveat). An explicit `captionFont` wins with the normal restyle rules. The [approved portrait reference](../assets/b1/portrait-approved-2026-11-13.mp4) uses the genuinely approved Chalkboard/`Hand` restyle for both scene text and captions; do not substitute Noto and claim to reproduce that look. Preserve the accepted handwritten treatment when continuing it. Apple font files are not bundled. OFL Caveat is a portable handwritten fallback candidate requiring actual encoded hierarchy/fit and phone-size review, not an automatic equivalence claim. Apply the [native portrait contract](b1-b2-b3-native-portrait.md) to per-shot composition, safe areas, visible local action and independent normal-speed phone-size review. Recompose for the requested ratio/language; retain handwritten scene headings as the stronger focal point without replacing access captions. Natural semantic breaks and actual-word timing come from the B common checklist, not a fixed word-count split. A necessary language glyph fallback retains the ratio-specific size; record it or a genuine user-approved restyle rather than silently substituting or shrinking type.

Sample voice credits: Ryan V4 uses Google Gemini Bodi; the earlier V15 uses Google Gemini Sadaltager. These are references, not prerequisites: honor the user's selected voice/service and help configure it if needed. Preserve accepted audio at native rate. Use a quiet story-appropriate continuous bed with restrained original/licensed, action-specific taps, paper, reveals, impacts or transitions; motivated AUDIO CUT is allowed. Mix below intelligible narration; BGM is optional, not A's mandatory piano. Listen to the actual output; technical checks cannot prove the sound matches the baseline.

## B1 reference comparison and full-film handoff

Apply the common delivery gate to every shot, not just the approved sample. Compare B1 character proportions/expressions, prop contact, background, text hierarchy, screenshot framing, local motion and sound detail through body/end against the accepted opening and references. Watch/listen to actual rendered output; a contact sheet cannot establish timing or sound. Retain the common ledger, failing timestamps, QA results and fingerprint-based preservation record, fixing only affected shots/cues.

The [long-form evidence manifest](../assets/b1/approved-production-standard.json) records the user's accepted B1 quality reference alongside Ryan V4. Its sampled sheet illustrates composition/detail, not a fixed storyboard or proof of continuous motion. Those assets, B1-specific design and voice defaults are not inherited by A/other B styles; the separately required B common quality gates still apply to every B production. Follow Autopilot's existing sample/authorization gates; no automatic paid retry, upload, showcase replacement or GitHub publication follows.
