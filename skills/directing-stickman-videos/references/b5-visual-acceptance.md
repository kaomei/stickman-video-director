# B5 optional strict visual acceptance: candidate, evidence, independent review

## Scope: explicit formal QA, not the default preview

Ordinary requested previews use the [default first-preview workflow](b5-first-preview.md): one actual candidate playback review, compact findings, and local repair. Do not load this whole evidence protocol or require its receipts merely to make a 10-second preview. Use the protocol below when the user explicitly requests strict independent QA or a stated delivery contract requires formal evidence-bound acceptance. Its stricter checks never imply user acceptance.

## Strict first review and local repair

选择严格模式后，首次审查完整执行下文的独立 image-first → seal → phase2 路径。已有真实审查后的局部姿态或形状修复可执行 `png-revision-review-pack`，再向 `png-review-check` 或 `encode` 提供 `--png-revision REVISION_JSON` 和当前 `--png-evidence`。revision 不要求重演盲读，也不把旧 overall unknown/fail 改成 pass。

工具完整核验当前项目、全部帧 ledger、当前证据及父 evidence/first-read/seal/phase2 的实际文件 SHA；逐项依赖包括完整图、neutral crop、必要状态和相邻图，只有先前该单项 pass 且依赖未变才可继承。全帧差异用于失效判断，不能只相信作者申报或少数抽样。脚本原文、时序、计划、布局或裁剪变化须重新全量审查；旧包没有记录任意布局源码 hash，故不声称已经机械证明源码未变，实际审核者还须明确确认局部修复范围。

增量 pack 自动产生 dependency/reuse 表与一个短确认模板。仍由同一实际独立审核者，将已有 canonical delta JSON 以 path+SHA 引用，并确认语义输入未改、原阻塞已在当前上下文解决。delta 中所见完整图 SHA 必须覆盖变更采样、边界邻图及每个原 unknown/fail 的所需上下文；不能继承 unknown/fail，也不能伪造重新看过的未变段。具体且不改含义的细节可保留 unknown、blocking:false；主体解剖、支撑、动作含义、字幕或证据不足的关键 unknown 仍阻塞。

直接导出 wrapper 必须将更新后的 `png-review-check ... --png-revision REVISION_JSON` 作为 FFmpeg 前的最后一个进程命令；check 非零即停止。此收据只允许当前内部编码，不表示旧冷启动完整链通过、用户认可或当前 MP4 已完整播放/听音。

This optional strict protocol applies to the **new B5 candidate selected for formal acceptance**, in any language and native aspect ratio. It strengthens the existing B5 route; it does not create another Skill or change B1–B4. Existing immutable, actually accepted baseline composites retain their historical acceptance. Reusing their assets or code does not transfer acceptance to a new encode.

## Separate technical success from visual acceptance

`check`, `validate` and `frames` establish only their stated technical checks. Hashes, fixed-length bones, a valid cue ledger, changing pixels and a successful encode cannot establish a good visual-event relay, causal acting, natural arms or readable animated captions. Do not describe any of them as visual approval.

`encode` creates a candidate MP4 and `candidate.json` with status `pending_visual_review`; it does **not** create `delivery.json`. An optional `--approval-note` records a real request or approval origin, with its actual scope. It cannot approve unseen media, certify review or change candidate status. Never fabricate one to make a command run.

The route is **study latest filled Mei example + approved relay film + CrazyMei + Spider → independent actual-storyboard rehearsal → pre-code plan-lock → author → actual final-PNG review → encode internal candidate → extract evidence → independent actual-media review → preview-ready → user preview / full accept**. Follow the [strict recipe below](#strict-execution-recipe) for the rehearsal, checkpoint and preview-review schema. Before first encode, perform its [current-final-PNG two-stage review](#review-the-exact-final-pngs-before-encode): a fresh reviewer first sees only derived neutral stage crops and saves/seals that reading, then the same reviewer receives plans and exact full compositions, including adjacent caption reveal/settled and replacement states. The pack records full PNG hashes, crop derivation and any explicit diagnostic masks; masked pictures, plan text or an old render cannot prove what the final composition displays or clears. Generated templates start unknown. All action, full-composition, caption-card and adjacent-card results require actual image observations; a seal alone does not pass. Both native ratios use the same three primary quality targets (latest approved Mei relay, CrazyMei and Spider); Jensen is historical only. A film remains pending whenever review is missing, unavailable, inconclusive or failing. Technical quality, independent visual acceptance and final user acceptance are separate records. The user’s approval of a face, script, preview, budget or previous version must not be relabeled as approval of the final film.

## Plan complete coverage before extraction

The pre-encode neutral first-read seal preserves a completed observation record, including honest uncertainty or failure; it is not visual approval. Its contextual phase must account for every uncertain/failed reading using the same actual image and available preceding/following images. Reasonable transitional fragments and a still's evidence limits can be nonblocking when the observed sequence supports that judgment. Ambiguous principal shapes, missing body/prop connection and failed contact remain defects; intent text or a small inset cannot repair the dominant picture. Do not erase or relabel the first reading to obtain a pass. This distinction does not waive the continuous encoded evidence below.

Keep `scene/visual-plan.json` tied to the approved original-speed voice, actual word-timing transcript and exact native ratio. Use the supported schema below. It must cover:

- Every main left-stage event, with stable ID, narration anchor, continuous interval and kind. Use `physical` for actual force/contact acting, with six positions: **before, anticipation, contact, extreme consequence, delayed response, recovery**. Use explicit `visual` for graphic reveals, replacements, transformations, emphasis and transitions, with meaningful ordered visual samples. Record the main visual before/after and handoff, not fictional body mechanics. Include actor/support/grip/deformation details wherever present. Event intervals together cover the **entire timeline**, with no gap larger than one project frame. Include readable holds in their event intervals and describe their purpose/exit; do not select only successful clips or invent motion to fill a hold.
- Every caption group, with its actual text, language, speech interval and language-appropriate semantic reveal units. Tie each unit to consecutive source-word indices in the locked actual-voice transcript. Across the complete plan, these indices must cover the source exactly once, in order, with no omitted, duplicate or reordered token. Define entrance, meaningful emphasis and readable hold within those intervals; verify their actual development in continuous playback, not from the sample labels alone. Headings are separate editorial elements.
- The full encoded film, so the reviewer can inspect all transitions, uncovered moments, overall pacing, composition, sound and continuity. Neither a contact sheet nor selected clips establish that the entire film was watched.

The technical production manifest’s motion `reason`, `semantic_review` and `sound_review` text remain useful context. They are **not** substitutes for review-plan coverage, extracted media files or recorded observations from actual playback.

## Build evidence from the final candidate

Run `review-pack` against the candidate directory. Extract from the **final encoded MP4**, not newly rendered substitute pictures, a separate checker animation or an earlier preview:

- For every main event: kind-specific samples (six physical phase frames, or ordered labeled visual frames) and a continuous **silent left-stage crop** with lead-in/out. Watch the interval at normal speed after the samples. The crop must contain its complete main visual, actors/props where present and all extremes; hiding a bad contact, unreadable path or failed transition invalidates the evidence.
- For every caption group: before/reveal frames for each unit, peak/settle sample frames for marked emphasis, plus a continuous caption-region clip **with the candidate’s actual audio**. Inspect actual entrance, emphasis and readable hold throughout that clip; a sample named “peak” does not establish that the renderer produced one. A static glyph image proves neither animated reveal nor speech timing.
- The candidate itself for complete audiovisual playback. Local clips diagnose a beat; they do not replace full-film review.

Preserve `evidence.json`, exact candidate/project/plan/transcript/file hashes and command outputs. The ledger's `source-video` entry references the complete candidate path and hash; its generated full-stage silent and full-caption audio crops support complete playback. Artifact IDs and paths come from that ledger; do not invent them. Evidence from a source render is insufficient when the final MP4 differs. Do not edit extracted files, replace a clip with a better take or trim away a failing phase while retaining its old manifest.

Keep candidate, evidence, review and delivery files **outside the production project directory**. In particular, write the independent `review.json` outside the project: the project fingerprint includes its files, so adding a review report there would change the reviewed input. All command output directories must be fresh; preserve prior versions rather than overwriting them.

## Review record and binding

`scene/visual-plan.json` uses these fields. Times are final-video seconds; rectangles are `[left, top, width, height]` in the native frame. Use real values from the approved voice and candidate, never sample timestamps:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| `schema`, `author_id` | `1` and the actual author identity, kept distinct from the independent reviewer. |
| `transcript` | `{path, sha256}` locking the actual word-timing source, with `path` relative to `scene/` (for example `word-timings.json`). Its `words` carry `text`/`screen` text and `start`/`end` times; keep the actual accepted narration. |
| `stage_region` | The left-stage review crop, including all relevant bodies, props, contact points and extremes. |
| `actions[]` shared fields | Unique `id`, `start`, `end`, `intent`; intervals cover the entire film. `kind` is `physical` or `visual`. Omitted `kind` remains legacy `physical`. |
| Physical action | `actor_region` and `phases`: strictly increasing actual times for `before`, `anticipation`, `contact`, `consequence`, `delayed_response`, `recovery`, mapping to six distinct final-video frames. `consequence` is the extreme. Keep real force/body/contact checks. |
| Visual event | `kind: "visual"`, `event_region`, concrete `movement`, and `visual_change: {before, after}` with distinct actual visible states. `visual_phases` is an ordered array of `{label, at}`, at least two samples, first label `before`, last `after`; meaningful labels are unique, 1–64 safe ASCII letters/digits/hyphen/underscore, start alphanumeric and cannot be `continuous`. Times stay inside the event and map to distinct final-video frames. Add peak/handoff samples when useful; no invented contact/arms. A hold belongs to the event whose before/after change it lets us read. |
| `captions.language`, `captions.region` | Actual language/script context and the complete caption-region crop. |
| `captions.groups[]` | Unique `id`, `start`, `end`, and semantic `units`. Include every group in the film. |
| `captions.groups[].units[]` | `text`, `start`, `end`, `emphasis` (boolean), and `word_indices` for consecutive actual transcript tokens. The text must match those source tokens after NFC/whitespace normalization; all flattened indices must equal the complete source order exactly. Reveal start must match the first source word within one frame, and end must cover the final source word while remaining inside its group. |

The external `review.json` uses `schema: 1` and:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| `reviewer` | Actual `id`, `kind` (`human` or `agent`), actual inspection `method`, `independent: true`. The reviewer ID must differ from `author_id`; distinct labels alone do not make a self-review independent. |
| `video_sha256`, `evidence_sha256` | Hashes of the actual candidate MP4 and exact `evidence.json` bytes. |
| `baseline_comparisons[]` | All three actually inspected primary films: `id` (`mei-event-relay`, `crazymei` and `spiderman`), `source_path`, `source_sha256`, and concrete `observations`. Record unavailable additional comparisons honestly. |
| `full_playback` | `silent_stage`, `complete_mix`, `captions_with_audio`, each with `status`, actual playback `observations` and `viewed_ranges: [{start, end}]`. Each item's merged viewed ranges must cover `0` through the candidate duration at project-frame precision; selected moments do not establish complete playback. |
| `actions[]` | Every planned event `id`. Physical checks: `causality`, `body_motion`, `arms_pose`, `contact`, `effects`, `recovery`, `pacing`. Visual checks: `meaningful_change`, `composition`, `richness`, `effects`, `readability`, `pacing`. Use the matching kind, not fake physical observations for graphics. |
| `captions[]` | Every planned group `id`, with `checks` for `segmentation`, `reveal`, `emphasis`, `shaping`, `timing`, `readability`. |
| `blocking_issues` | Actual unresolved blockers. It must be empty to accept. |

Every result has `status` and `observations`; each observation has `at` (actual candidate seconds), `detail` (what was seen/heard) and `evidence_ids` (existing artifact IDs from `evidence.json`). Action/group observations must belong to their actual intervals. Every action criterion must include an observation referencing its `action-<id>-continuous` clip, and every caption criterion its `caption-<id>-continuous` clip; phase PNGs may provide additional evidence but cannot alone establish pacing, reveal or continuous behavior. Use the exact artifact IDs in the ledger. Pure status words such as “pass”, “ok”, “通过”, “正常” or “已检查” are rejected as observations; describe the actual visible/audible result, without padding to an arbitrary word count.

To prepare a review, initialize an unperformed action/caption check as `{"status":"unknown","observations":[]}` and each unperformed full-playback item as `{"status":"unknown","observations":[],"viewed_ranges":[]}`. Replace them only after real inspection; an empty or preset passing report cannot be accepted. Do not copy another film's timestamps, source hash, artifact IDs, viewed ranges or observations.

`review-pack` supplies `review-template.json` with unknown results and empty observations. Copy it to an external `review.json` for the independent reviewer to complete; do not edit the hashed evidence files or mistake the template's existence for a pass.

## Independent reviewer: observe, then decide

A human or a separate reviewing Agent must review in two stages. First, before seeing the script, approved brief, plans/cards, intended nouns or prior verdicts, actually view neutral-ID main-picture images extracted from this encoded candidate, with explanatory titles, captions, intent labels and descriptive filenames hidden; retain words that are themselves the event. Save an unprompted reading of subject, relation, visible action/state and alternate readings. Use the [image-first record and status rules](#independent-storyboard-rehearsal-before-plan-lock); rehearsal IDs come from `storyboard.images`, while this candidate's working record must bind to its actual extracted-image IDs. Cover each event's result, and never replace the saved first reading with a later corrected one. Use a fresh reviewer who has not already been given this render's intended reading. Every actual render needs new observation, not “unchanged relative to v3” or inheritance of an old pass.

Second, reveal the real files, approved brief, action cards and language context. Compare the saved reading with intent and record whether it needed correction, then actually inspect the evidence, latest approved Mei event-relay, CrazyMei and Spider complete composites and the complete encoded film. The authoring Agent, renderer, test output or a generated checklist cannot self-certify the result. Reviewer identity/type and actual playback coverage must be recorded; an Agent must use available visual/audio capabilities rather than infer quality from code, metadata or filenames. PNG recognition and relay observations do not certify complete motion, pacing or sound; unperformed AV checks remain `unknown`.

Record concrete observations per action and caption group, including where in the evidence they were observed. Mark a criterion `pass` only after observing it. Use `fail` for a visible defect and `unknown` for an unavailable, ambiguous or unperformed check. Record open issues and unavailable source comparisons honestly. An `unknown`, `fail`, missing item, blocking issue or incomplete full-film playback prevents acceptance. Never paste preset “all pass” wording or copy another version’s review.

### Every main event must pass

- **Event relay and meaning:** compare each event's actual pictured lead versus subordinate context, and the whole dominant-picture sequence, with the source images and independently rehearsed storyboard. Identify silhouette/scale/hierarchy evidence: a named lead in JSON or a correct local gesture cannot pass a macro relay when the old full-actor tableau still dominates. Each conceptual handoff needs a visible new lead, representation or relationship. Judge this handoff separately from the event's visible state/action change and explanatory result; neither observation substitutes for the other. A camera-size change alone is local coverage; one actor/tableau under zooms, repeated action or new labels does not establish a relay. Graphic/part-only scenes may take over with compact recognizable ownership cues; retaining meaning does not require the whole actor in every beat. Check actual before/result/next-visual images and continuous playback; storyboard rehearsal is no proof that the renderer implemented it, and no fixed event quota applies.
- **Whole-duration development:** inspect through the requested picture end, including any time after supplied speech ends. The ending must follow the current meaning through supported action/consequence, delayed tails and a readable result, or an appropriate next semantic handoff. Comparison/order graphics need an explanatory purpose in the current words; an otherwise unneeded side-by-side recap, chain or summary added to occupy seconds fails. Parking the same tableau for leftover runtime fails pacing/development; an earned result hold remains valid. Do not require unsupported events, motives or an arbitrary number of new facts to fill time.
- **Visual events:** inspect the declared transformation/reveal/path, visual hierarchy, relevant secondary motion, readable result/hold and exit. Words/graphics may be the main event. A nonphysical event does not need fictional force or a figure, but its pictures must actually implement the stated change. Do not use `kind: visual` to exempt actual physical action inside the event from anatomy/contact review.
- **Referent and relation:** compare the saved pre-intent reading of small before/result/handoff thumbnails from the final MP4 with the plan's `referent`, `preserved_identity_cues` and visible claim. Crops, enlargements and substitutions meant to depict the same subject must retain distinguishing contours and connecting context for its ownership/support/contact. Prefer the established drawing components and pose transforms with compact, readable body connections; semantic clarity takes priority over removing all human context. A significant alternate-object reading or unresolved relation must fail or remain unknown, even if an old report passed or an earlier full actor, fine connector or inset suggests the intended answer. Those cues must actually resolve the current dominant image at delivery size. A correct label cannot repair the silhouette. Check the actual continuous handoff as well; the plan's words do not prove continuity.
- **Investigation and information gain:** compare the visible states with the planned `visible_information_gain`. A relevant observable state, temporal relationship or unresolved comparison must become legible. A magnifier, arrow or time label on an unchanged tableau cannot alone establish that result. Reusing an earlier picture can support a new visible order/comparison; do not demand an invented action, fact or motive. Preserve the spoken uncertainty. Record defects under the existing meaning/composition/readability or physical causality checks, not a new preset passing field.

For physical events, check causality, force/contact and recovery. For **any figures present**, additionally check ordinary source proportions, silhouette, anatomy and natural hands; their mere presence does not require a collision or six invented physics phases:

- **Causality and state change:** setup, anticipation, contact/constraint, consequence, delayed response and recovery read as one chain. In a physical event the actor’s body, balance, location, silhouette or freedom visibly changes. Prop entrance, a tiny body pulse and a generic burst fail even when pixels move.
- **Force, contact and effects:** weight and direction are legible; hand/prop/ground contacts agree; material responses and secondary motion follow the contact. Effects support that force and consequence. Floating grip, teleportation, uniform prop bounces, unexplained shake or effects covering missing acting fail.
- **Natural arms and hands:** review the anonymous left actors as well as the presenter. A left actor must not keep both arms raised, pinned to one elbow angle or repeating the same wave throughout unrelated beats. Each raised/reaching/held pose needs a visible task, support or force and a credible entry, follow-through and exit. Hands return to a motivated rest or the next task; a sustained hanging, resisting or trapped pose may persist only while its constraint remains legible. Review shoulder/body participation, bend direction, reach and hand ownership throughout the clip. Arbitrary long arms, duplicate limbs, sliding shoulders and unmotivated rigid symmetry fail. Normal stillness is allowed; random motion is not a repair.
- **Recovery and staging:** intentional squash/stretch has clean entry, extreme and recovery or a story-justified retained state. All extremes, actor sizes, titles, captions and presenter remain legible and uncropped. A long hold must serve the scene and lead to a changed next state.

### Every caption group must pass, in every language

- **Semantic rhythm and continuity:** group at actual speech pauses and complete semantic dependencies. Preserve noun phrases, modifiers, verbs with necessary objects, phrasal verbs, names and number–unit relationships. Do not clear a preposition or subordinate introducer before its continuation arrives; a small timing gap does not repair that dependency. A long clause can use a meaningful line/reveal break within the same group, or split at a genuine semantic break. Do not split by fixed characters, words or duration, or force every language into English space-delimited tokens. Inspect adjacent actual full-composition states across every group replacement, not only isolated caption samples or transcript coverage. For example, `Wait until / the light turns green` may reveal in one group; clearing `Wait until` before replacing it with `the light turns green` strands the dependent thought.
- **Progressive reveal:** reveal semantic units in the actual voice rhythm, with visible entrance and development. A complete static sentence throughout the group, arbitrary per-character flashing or one reused bounce detached from speech fails the animation gate.
- **Meaningful emphasis and hold:** the key word or phrase receives a readable, motivated emphasis; the viewer can see it develop and then settle. Keep enough hold to read the thought. Constant popping, equal emphasis on everything, abrupt disappearance and effect-driven obstruction fail.
- **Language shaping:** use the actual script and a licensed font with the required glyphs. Inspect CJK glyphs and punctuation, Arabic joining and bidirectional order, combining marks/diacritics or other relevant shaping. Reveal complete shaped semantic units; do not fragment a grapheme, joined word, proper name or number–unit pair to imitate a reference effect. Missing glyph boxes, separated marks, wrong reading order and substituted example words fail.
- **Timing and readability:** match the approved audio, including pauses and transitions; no omitted, duplicated, reordered or overlapping words. Inspect every entrance/emphasis/hold for safe bounds, contrast, scale, unobscured text and B5’s maximum three semantic lines. Typography overrides and language fallback preserve these requirements.

The reviewer must also check the complete encoded film with sound: presenter identity and acting, narration continuity, material SFX at contacts, speech intelligibility, native-ratio composition and transitions. Record actual listening; waveform analysis or metadata cannot substitute for it.

## Accept or return for local repair

Run `accept` only with the candidate, its extracted evidence directory, the completed independent review and `--preview-ready /absolute/preview-ready-directory`. The receipt must bind the same candidate/evidence and a genuinely pre-implementation plan checkpoint. Preview readiness is a prerequisite, not a substitute for the full action/caption review below. The command must reject mismatched fingerprints, altered/missing evidence, incomplete coverage, any non-pass criterion and unresolved blocking issues. Only then does it create `delivery.json` with `status: visual_review_passed` and `user_acceptance: not_inferred`, bound to the exact reviewed bytes and report. A technical command cannot authenticate a reviewer’s honesty; truthful actual-media review remains mandatory.

Any change to the candidate, relevant project input, review plan, evidence or review invalidates the old acceptance binding. Repair the failed interval or language treatment within the user’s scope, reuse unaffected approved assets/audio, encode a new candidate and rebuild/review the affected evidence plus the resulting full film. Do not mark an old report current by renaming a file or copying its approval text.

If a reviewer cannot watch or hear the required material, leave the candidate pending and state the missing check. A successful `accept` records independent visual acceptance; record the final user’s real response separately when it exists. Do not claim the user has accepted an unseen film. Deliver the MP4/preview together with its verified absolute folder link, review status and remaining gaps; publishing still requires its own authorization.


## Strict execution recipe

The following detailed recipe is retained for explicit strict QA only. Its pre-encode and independent review receipts are not default-preview prerequisites.

## 1. Study the actual targets before choosing choreography

The [latest user-approved filled Mei example](b5-mei-filled-example.md) **and its [exact 11.37-second approved film](../assets/b5/local-baselines/mei-event-relay/approved-sample.mp4) are mandatory on first use and every new B5 story**. Read the example in full and visually study the actual encoded film before choosing the new choreography. It is the concrete effect target for successive main visuals; reading only the original prompt or blank template misses this step. The [approval record](../assets/b5/local-baselines/mei-event-relay/README.md) defines its scope and limits.

Before composing the current story, also read the [worked takeover mechanism/source entry](../examples/b5-mei-event-relay/README.md) and its linked actual implementation beside the corresponding reference images. Study how the former leading group exits or becomes subordinate while an independently composed lead takes over. Transfer that implementation mechanism to the current script; the phone gag and its exact choreography are not defaults. Code study does not replace viewing the actual film or certify a new output.

`init` returns `required_first_use` paths/hashes for the filled example, original prompt, latest film and approval record; its `approved-reference.mp4` is now the latest relay film. After initialization, run:

```sh
node scripts/character-explainer.mjs reference-study /absolute/project --output /absolute/reference-study-v1
```

This extracts twelve actual relay frames, a board and a manifest, and writes the project `reference-study.json` and unperformed `relay-rehearsal.json` templates. Read the complete filled example and inspect the actual reference evidence. Inspect the real reference's setup, decisive changes, results and handoffs—not only a poster or the code. Record actual timestamps, the leading visual/scale, movement versus read intervals, what result remains visible, and how the next leading visual takes over. State whether observations came from playback or decoded frames; a contact sheet is not proof of watching or hearing the complete movie. If playback is unavailable, perform the available decoded visual study, disclose that limit, and retain unknown AV results rather than skipping the latest reference or claiming a full pass.

Use the project's generated `reference-study.json` to record the study honestly. After actual inspection, set `status: observed`, select the real `viewing_mode` (`decoded_frames`, `continuous_playback` or `both`) and state `listening_performed` truthfully. Fill the six **source-reference** beat observations with observed `lead_visual`, `visual_change`, `timing` and `handoff`; those six source beats are not a required event count for the new film.

Transfer mechanisms, scale contrast, short decisive changes and readable results to the current-script plan. Complete `current_story_sequence` for every visual-plan action with `action_id`, `lead_visual`, `representation`, `change_from_previous`, `handoff`, `hold_reason`, `referent`, `preserved_identity_cues` and `visible_information_gain`. `referent` names the pictured subject/relation; `preserved_identity_cues` names the actual contours and connecting context that will keep it recognizable through a crop, enlargement or substitution, including ownership/support/contact where relevant. `visible_information_gain` names the actual state, temporal relationship or uncertainty the viewer will be able to see. Explain how spoken ideas hand the main stage to those representations and why a reading hold is needed. These are planned semantic claims, not proof that the pictures implement them; filling strings does not pass visual review. Keep every source/evidence binding unchanged. The command's generated files do not attest that the author inspected them; the plan lock checks the study record and its bindings before implementation.

Also read the [baseline registry](b5-accepted-baselines.md), [left-stage action contract](b5-left-stage-motion.md), [avatar contract](b5-avatar-motion.md), [sound contract](b5-sound-qa.md) and [acceptance protocol](b5-visual-acceptance.md). Watch both [CrazyMei](../assets/b5/local-baselines/crazymei/approved-sample.mp4) and [Spider](../assets/b5/approved-sample.mp4), not only their posters; they remain the drawing, staging and physical-acting masters in both ratios. Read the verified original Mei prompt, then inspect implementation or physical strips where useful. Do not copy a reference account, identity, script, default voice or phone/coffee gag into an unrelated story.

Judge the same qualities in either ratio:

| Quality | What must be visible | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drawing and staging | Warm paper, decisive dark contours, restrained red; actor/prop silhouettes read at normal viewing size; stage has a clear foreground action. | Tiny actors along the bottom of a mostly empty canvas; thin stock icons, crowded labels or UI cards standing in for acting. |
| Main visual sequence | Voice-aligned visual verbs lead from one major event to the next through reveal, transformation, replacement, physical gag or graphic path. | One extended minor-movement scene when an event relay is requested; unrelated icons surrounding an idle figure. |
| Physical acting where present | Setup → anticipation → contact/constraint → changed body/state → delayed response → recovery. | A prop appears, actor pulses, generic burst; contact has no consequence. |
| Richness | Main-visual handoffs, relevant secondary/material/graphic motion, varied silhouettes and shot scales; readable results and exits. | More unrelated objects, confetti, event counts, camera shake or the same jump replayed. |
| Timing | A readable preparation/hold, uneven acceleration, decisive contact, delayed follow-through and a motivated exit. | Constant even-speed tweening, frantic motion everywhere, or filler stillness. |
| Limbs and contact | Normal head/trunk/limb proportions and line weight match the chosen source model; ordinary bones stay stable; the wrist owns its prop; tasks motivate bends/reaches and rest; comic deformation is bounded and recovers. | Raised arms throughout unrelated beats, sliding grips, duplicate limbs or accidental elastic elbows. |
| Whole composition | Stage, editorial title, presenter and semantic animated captions share one readable frame; sound contacts reinforce the actual action. | Attractive presenter/captions masking weak left-stage animation, or a crop hiding the contact/extreme. |

These approved complete references are landscape. Historical portrait films/templates also exist, but do not transfer acceptance to a new portrait. Apply the **same quality standard**, author native geometry and review it independently. In portrait, use vertical trajectories/depth and reserve readable caption/presenter space; do not simply scale the landscape stage down or crop it. If both ratios are requested, reuse approved identity, narration and semantic event timing; author and review each layout separately.

## 2. Design before implementing the new stage

First lock the [actual ordinary actor model](b5-avatar-motion.md#lock-the-anonymous-actor-model-before-new-choreography). Put source/new neutral and key task poses side by side at the same head diameter before tweening; preserve relative limb/trunk lengths, line weight, support and readable silhouette. Run the small source-rig demonstration if the drawing has not been studied. A new renderer must port the drawing, not only implement a fixed-bone solver. A wrong normal model is an internal failure before action richness is judged.

Read the [verified original Mei prompt](b5-mei-original-prompt.md), fill the [Mei scene template](b5-mei-scene-template.md), then apply the [directing structure](b5-crazymei-directing-structure.md). Plan the succession of main visuals before refining local mechanics. Each scene has Voiceover / On-Screen Text / multi-step Animation Action / SFX. A new metaphor, graphic, transformation or prop can take over the stage; do not impose one occupied task and retained constraint across the film. The [V5 case](b5-v5-causal-workflow.md) is optional for local contact/timing study, with historical proportions that are not the model master.

For each event, specify its semantic trigger, leading visual change, entrance/path/curve, readable result and handoff/exit. Add independently delayed tails when useful. Use six physical phases for actual force/contact acting; graphic reveals and transformations use meaningful visual samples. Put the choices in existing cards/plans, without another user approval form.

Before code, make small storyboard thumbnails of the principal before/result/handoff states. Use the two-stage image-first rehearsal below: save an unprompted reading before revealing the intended claim, then compare it with the plan. Hide explanatory titles, captions and diagnostic labels; retain words that are themselves the event. A cropped or enlarged referent must retain its distinguishing contours and enough connecting context to preserve the intended relation. Prefer reusing the established drawing components and pose transforms, with compact, readable body/ownership connections, over redrawing a part as an unrelated geometric symbol. Keep enough human context to make the picture clear; removing all of it is not a goal. Record corrections in the existing cards and `current_story_sequence`, rather than using their prose to excuse an ambiguous drawing.

Keep identity/ownership cues compact: an established part, object or graphic may become the dominant picture without retaining the whole actor and room. Any visible ordinary figure still uses the Mei drawing model. Camera scale alone is local coverage; at each conceptual handoff the picture must visibly change its lead, representation or relationship. A board dominated by the same actor/tableau, with zooms, repeated action or changing labels, has not established the requested relay.

Test actual hierarchy, not a declaration that something is “large”: which silhouette takes precedence at a delivery-size thumbnail, where does the eye land, and is the supposed new lead still a small detail inside the former main scene? At takeover, exit, replace or demote the former main group; use only the context fragments needed to identify a part or its ownership/contact. A full owner is not default context for every event. The new lead's scale, contrast and placement must carry its stated role in the actual picture.

For an investigation, rewind or time-order view, identify the relevant observable difference or relationship the picture will expose. It may clarify an already shown state, the order of events or an unresolved comparison; it need not add a new physical action. A magnifier, arrow or `BEFORE` label over an otherwise unchanged tableau does not establish new evidence by itself. Preserve the script's factual limits and uncertainty; do not invent a cause, motive or event just to make the reveal look busier.

Use actual approved speech and its word timing. If speech is not yet available, make a clearly provisional storyboard and resolve only the missing voice/cost choice under existing authorization; never invent actual timing or buy an audition automatically. Initialize the external project before editing its implementation. Default B5 `init` keeps the shared renderer infrastructure but installs the neutral [new-story stage API](../examples/b5-mei-event-relay/new-story-stage.cjs); its scene deliberately throws until the current story is authored after the planning gates. It supplies no sample choreography. The historical Spider scene is available only through explicit `init <project> --reference-scaffold` for inspection/fidelity work, not as the new-story default.

Plan caption groups at actual speech pauses and complete semantic dependencies. Preserve noun phrases, modifiers, verbs with necessary objects, phrasal verbs, names and number–unit pairs. Do not clear a preposition or subordinate introducer before its necessary continuation appears. For example, clearing `Check the seal before` and replacing it with `you open the lid` breaks the dependency; a single group can progressively reveal `Check the seal / before you open the lid`, with `/` a line break rather than a cleared group. Likewise, keep `pick up the glass` together rather than clearing `pick` before `up the glass`. Fit a long clause through a meaningful line/reveal break within its group or a genuine semantic break, never a fixed word, character or duration count. A small timing gap alone does not make an incomplete dependency a valid group.

Write the filled scene template, action cards, `scene/visual-plan.json` and `scene/preview-plan.json` before implementation. Cover every spoken idea and the whole timeline, including planned holds and transitions. Choose event developments by meaning, without a fixed quota. When successive effects are requested, the storyboard must show main-visual handoffs; one continuous task with small pose changes is an internal directing failure. A purposeful physical sequence may span a clause, but is not the default for the whole preview.

Mark the actual speech end separately from the requested picture end. Plan the last supported action, consequence, delayed tails and result read across that time while preserving the original voice speed. Unused seconds do not justify a new comparison, side-by-side summary or collected-object chain. Use such a relation only when it explains the current words, and a next semantic handoff only when it belongs in the requested excerpt. Do not append a passive same-scene hold merely because speech has finished. A purposeful result hold remains valid; there is no required number of new events or facts, and unsupported behavior or motives must not be invented to fill time.

Use the latest filled example to test the storyboard, not to swap its nouns. For each planned handoff, put **leading image before → leading image after → spoken meaning gained** in the cards. If the only difference is the same figure in the same furniture with another small pose, change the representation where the meaning allows it. Close-ups, object-only events, transformations and explanatory graphics can lead the whole stage. A second performance of an earlier action needs a new visible explanatory result; replaying it under a new heading does not create richness. Keep decisive results large enough to read; do not shrink the conclusion into a tiny row merely to fill leftover time. Choose event count and hold lengths from meaning, voice and reading need, not a quota.

For example, a signal can rush into frame, hand the stage to a magnified gaze, trigger a brief collision, then yield to a spill freeze and a graphic reordering of earlier/later events. Each visual carries the spoken explanation. Within a collision, preserve actual contact, force and consequence. Decorative icon entrances around an idle figure are insufficient, as is holding the same domestic scene for the entire script when the brief requests effect after effect.

### Independent storyboard rehearsal before plan-lock

Use an available collaboration tool to start a fresh independent reviewer with no authoring-chat history. Review in two ordered stages; do not supply a preferred verdict, prior diagnosis or extra director prompts.

1. **Initial read, before intent:** give the reviewer only the actual neutral-ID whole-sequence board and individual main-picture images, with explanatory titles, captions, intent labels and descriptive filenames hidden; retain words that are themselves the visual event. Do not reveal the script, brief, plans, intended nouns, action descriptions or old pass reports yet. The reviewer actually views the images and saves `image_first_reading` before receiving those materials. For each image, name the subject, visible ownership/support/contact relation, observed action or state, and plausible alternate readings. A still or strip supports only the states/changes it shows, not an inferred continuous action. Record unclear or competing readings as `fail` or `unknown`; never prefill `pass`. A clear first reading is not yet a match to author intent.
2. **Context and target comparison:** only after that record is saved, reveal the current user brief, plans/cards, action IDs/times and the same installed B5 references, including actual target images from the latest approved relay, CrazyMei and Spider. Compare the saved reading with the intended claim and record whether context required a correction, what changed, and whether the picture needs repair. Inspect **each event's pictured lead versus subordinate context** and the **whole sequence through the requested end**. Judge main-picture relay separately from the visible state/action change and explanatory result; a new scale, graphic or label cannot by itself prove that result. Cite actual silhouette/scale/hierarchy evidence. No fixed scene count is required.

A significant alternate-object reading or lost ownership/contact must be repaired or remain `fail`/`unknown`. An old pass, an earlier full actor, a fine connector or an inset is not an exemption: current visible context must actually resolve the dominant picture at delivery size. A readable part-only composition remains valid; do not force a whole actor into every event. Preserve the initial reading and any later correction instead of rewriting it as if recognition had been immediate.

Complete the generated `relay-rehearsal.json` from that real review; no additional CLI command is needed. Preserve failed reports separately. Its required bindings and observations are:

| Fields | Record from the actual storyboard and review |
| --- | --- |
| `schema`, `project`, `author_id` | `1`, the absolute real project path and the actual plan author. |
| `reference_study_sha256`, `visual_plan_sha256`, `preview_plan_sha256` | Hashes of the current project study and the two `scene/` plans. |
| `storyboard.board`, `storyboard.images` | Board `{id: "board", path, sha256}` plus one image strip per action in plan order, each `{id, action_id, path, sha256}`. Paths are absolute image files; IDs are unique. A strip may show before/result/handoff together. |
| `reviewer` | The real distinct `id`, `independent: true`, `viewed_images: true`, only after actual inspection. |
| `image_first_reading` | The saved pre-intent record below, by the actual reviewer. Its neutral `image_id` values come from `storyboard.images`; cover at least the result image/strip of every event. |
| `first_read_dispositions` | A separate second-stage record for every initial `unknown` or `fail`, in image order; preserve the original reading and status. Use the mapping and evidence requirements below. |
| `target_comparison` | Latest relay `source_sha256` and `observations: [{at, detail}]` at actual source-film times. Describe visible comparison with the current storyboard. |
| `sequence_observations` | `[{detail, evidence_ids}]` citing the actual board ID, judging the whole dominant-picture succession. |
| `events` | Every `action_id` in order, actual `status` and `observations: [{detail, evidence_ids}]` citing that action's image ID. |
| `status`, `blocking_issues` | Set `pass` and `[]` only after a real passing review of the whole sequence and every event, with no unresolved issue. |

`image_first_reading` has this shape; replace the example content and status with actual observations:

```json
{"reviewer_id":"actual-reviewer-id","recorded_before_intent":true,"images":[{"image_id":"neutral-storyboard-image-id","subject":"observed subject","relation":"observed ownership/support/contact","visible_action":"observed action or state","alternate_readings":[],"status":"unknown"}]}
```

Image status is `pass`, `fail` or `unknown`. An empty `alternate_readings` is valid only when none was actually observed. In the existing `events[].observations` and `sequence_observations`, record the second-stage comparison and any correction to this initial reading. Recognition, relay, visible state/action change and target richness each need their own concrete observation; one passing dimension cannot stand in for another.

For every original nonpassing image, the same reviewer fills a separate `first_read_dispositions` item: `{image_id, initial_status, previous_image_id, next_image_id, status, observations: [{detail, evidence_ids}]}`. Keep `image_id` and `initial_status` identical to the saved reading; nearest neighbors follow `storyboard.images` order and are `null` at boundaries. Its concrete observations must cite the current image, the overview board and both available nearest neighbors. Distinguish uncertainty about withheld wording or an unseen fact from an actual unclear silhouette, ownership or contact. Context can establish that an unspecified question was intentionally open, for example; it cannot repair a genuinely ambiguous principal picture by naming the intended subject. Explain the actual pictured evidence and preserve both the original reading and this later judgment. Do not overwrite the initial statuses to satisfy the validator. An all-`pass` initial reading needs no dispositions; it still needs the full second-stage review.

The reviewer must return critical `pass`, `fail` or `unknown` findings with concrete image evidence; restating the plan is insufficient. Only the final whole rehearsal, every event and every required disposition must pass, with no unresolved blocking issue. A final failed/unknown/unperformed rehearsal blocks plan-lock and implementation: revise the storyboard and its plans/study, then obtain an actual new review. Changed plans, study or images invalidate the old binding and require re-review. Do not invent a reviewer, rename the author as a second person or prefill a passing result. If independent review is unavailable, keep the rehearsal unperformed and the work internal; do not simulate a pass. The validator checks records and hashes, not beauty or honesty. Storyboard approval covers the planned still sequence only; it does not certify animation, listening or the eventual MP4.

Before changing choreography code, complete the required reference study and passing independent storyboard rehearsal, then run:

```sh
node scripts/character-explainer.mjs plan-lock /absolute/project --output /absolute/plan-v1
```

`init` records the initial code fingerprint. The first plan checkpoint must precede new implementation; existing code changes or stale render records cannot be retroactively called a pre-code plan. On a planned revision, first lock the revised plan with `--previous /absolute/plan-v1`, then edit the affected implementation. Keep checkpoints outside the project and preserve their chain. This records a local authoring sequence, not proof of honest watching or of visual quality.

For an older already-authored project, preserve the existing work, audio and media. Use it for diagnosis, then start a clean authoring receipt/checkpoint for the authorized changed implementation; do not replay paid calls or call a retrospective plan a first-use pass. Old candidates remain internal until they meet the current readiness requirements.

## 3. Author and inspect a candidate internally

Use the [internal repair loop](b5-v5-causal-workflow.md#internal-repair-loop-the-agent-owns-it) to catch and fix weak staging before exposing the first render. Preserve first-attempt observations and changes; do not wait for the user to request more motion.

Follow [installed execution](b5-character-explainer.md#installed-execution) and the [production manifest](b5-production-gate.md). Reuse authorized assets/audio. Apply the planned succession of main visuals, story-specific physical acting where present, readable graphic/actor scale, secondary action, actual event-driven sound and semantic captions. Look at stills/strips to locate defects, then watch continuous playback. Fix the failed beat; more props and generic bursts are not a repair.

### Review the exact final PNGs before encode

The first view is a neutral main-stage crop derived from the actual final PNG; the second is its exact native full composition. Neither view substitutes for the other.

Before the **first encode**, the contextual review must also inspect actual adjacent **full-composition rendered caption states** from the final PNG set: each group's reveal/settled state and the frames immediately before and after every group replacement. Read these with the locked actual speech text and timing, checking whether a necessary continuation survives the transition and remains readable with the stage, title and presenter. Masked main-stage images, `qualityFrame` output and plan text cannot establish caption grouping, clearing or composition. A separate reviewing Agent or human must first complete the fresh image-first reading above, then receive the real brief, action cards and evidence for context. A plan's phase labels or scene names do not establish that these results appeared.
