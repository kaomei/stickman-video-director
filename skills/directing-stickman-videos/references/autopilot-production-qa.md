# B · Autopilot shared production and acceptance

Required for **every B style**, before shot planning, sample review and full-film delivery. This is the common quality floor, not a shared drawing style. A does not inherit it. Each B style keeps its own character/visual language, caption design, pacing character and SFX treatment; those choices must still meet this checklist. B1/B2/B3/B5 showcase voice credits are illustrative: honor the user's selected available route and help configure it if needed. B4 keeps its scoped Brian contract. Future styles need their own reference and runtime support before use. Do not copy B1 assets, background, voice ID or typography into them.

For **B1/B2/B3**, also apply the mandatory [reference-effect acceptance protocol](b1-b2-b3-reference-acceptance.md). Complete production environments such as Codex and Claude Code must target actual sample-comparable design, acting, motion, editing, semantic captions and sound through the full film; fixtures and technical passes do not establish that result. Verify real capabilities. An observed tool limitation permits an explicitly limited best effort, with no sample-equivalent pass or forced unavailable/paid route. Preserve equivalent design and readable semantic timing across languages rather than imposing the identical English font.

For **B6**, also apply the strict [Sample 1 portrait V44 acceptance matrix](b6-sample-1-acceptance.md) and [motion execution/review gate](b6-motion-review.md). Its 35 independent subchecks cover actual drawing/light, matched-head-scale limb weight, all-cast continuous ownership and head/neck support, contact/performance, broad layered space, motivated camera, rhythm, exact portrait captions and original-speed sound. V90 is secondary engineering evidence. Compare real candidate/reference playback and native detail; geometry, preserved hashes, a plan label or an encode cannot establish a visual pass. Repair pending/needs-repair findings and repeat authorized local rendering/review rather than delivering a known failing test as completion. Keep technical results, independent creative review and user approval distinct.

## Plan from meaning and actual narration

Maintain a compact project-local shot ledger: spoken phrase and actual word times; idea to communicate; focal subject/action/expression; short scene text; timed meaningful picture events; evidence source/language/date; caption phrases; SFX event; and connection to the next shot. Cover the body and ending as well as the opening. This is production evidence, not another intake questionnaire.

Make actions, objects, diagrams and marks explain the spoken idea. A control needs visible operation/contact, a choice distinguishable alternatives, and an outcome a visible result. Preserve approved identities while varying acting and viewpoint; do not illustrate every sentence with a recycled presenter/laptop. Time reveals to the corresponding speech, allowing purposeful anticipation/reaction. Pictures follow the real voice; never stretch speech, add dead air or revoice accepted narration to fit a visual template.

## Native landscape and portrait

All B styles must support **16:9 and 9:16**. Honor a request for either ratio or both; resolve an unspecified delivery choice once without silently buying/rendering an extra version. A both-version request shares approved script, original-speed voice, accepted identity and reusable sound/assets, not one fixed composition.

Plan native stage, focal hierarchy, prop paths, typography, captions, presenter placement and platform-safe areas for each ratio. Re-author framing and motion where needed. Do not crop off meaningful actions, scale text to unreadability, stretch proportions, or describe a landscape crop as a validated portrait layout. A B5 portrait must preserve both the explaining stage and presenter without blindly copying its landscape x/y coordinates.

For **B1–B3 portrait**, apply the mandatory [native portrait contract](b1-b2-b3-native-portrait.md) before authoring: the `b123-native-portrait-v1` ratio-specific caption treatment, declared per-shot native composition regions and background exclusions, safe bounds and visible-action diagnostics, then independent 1× phone-size review covering the complete encoded scope and every shot. A candidate with readable numeric type size can still fail for composition, contact or platform occlusion. Compare the corresponding [approved internal 10-second portrait reference](b1-b2-b3-native-portrait.md) as well as the landscape source/style samples. Its user approval covers that exact clip's effect, not unseen full films or future outputs; no new export inherits it.

For **B6**, read the [native-format contract](b6-native-formats.md): portrait works best, while landscape/portrait each require their own storyboard, staging, contact/camera routes and caption composition. Use Sample 1 portrait's shared drawing/performance standard in either format; never crop a finished film or transfer approval between ratios. Verify the full authored world and extreme camera coverage before rendering.

Check representative opening/body/ending frames and actual moving playback in each requested ratio. Record resolution, safe-area/caption checks, action/presenter legibility, motion/contact checks and user-review status separately. Successful landscape encoding or a static avatar approval does not establish portrait capability. Existing untested style/ratio combinations remain explicit development/acceptance gaps; this requirement itself is not evidence they passed. This local rule update grants no new generation, paid calls, public sync or release authority.

## First 30 seconds: picture, sound and copy together

Give the **first 30 seconds**, or the whole opening when the video is shorter, a dedicated hook pass. Aim to substantially exceed a routine slideshow opening through relevance, clarity and coordinated picture/sound, not faster cuts or louder effects. Plan these beats explicitly in the ledger:

- When writing copy, start with a short, concrete line and an audience-relevant question, tension, surprising contrast or desired outcome. Show a relevant result, case or demonstration early; establish why to keep watching before lengthy setup. For a story, use its conflict rather than forcing a sales pitch. Avoid a generic greeting, logo hold or article screenshot as the entire hook.
- Match each line to a specific principal action, evidence reveal or visual payoff. Use story-specific expressions, scale/viewpoint changes and clear emphasis rather than an unrelated montage or the same animation held under a long sentence. Design the transition from hook to body so the later video actually delivers the opening promise.
- Place purposeful SFX at the important actions/reveals and review their timing with the words. Make the mix energetic or restrained as the selected style requires, always below intelligible narration; constant pops/whooshes are not polish.

If copy is supplied or already approved, preserve it; propose any needed hook rewrite for approval before changing the recording. Never invent earnings, popularity, testimonials or product capabilities to strengthen a hook, and do not promise virality. The normal approximately 20-second sample gate remains unchanged: review the remaining opening through 30 seconds during full production, without treating sample approval as proof of its quality.

## Meaningful motion, not filler

- No **principal-picture inactivity longer than 2.5 seconds** anywhere in a preview or finished film, except B4's explicit [deliberate-hold contract](b4-survival-storytelling.md#narration-motion-and-finishing). For B4 apply its deliberate-hold exception only to that shared 2.5-second inactivity convention through the required [per-shot picture plan, local animation and independent encoded review](b4-picture-animation.md): coherent held illustrations need narrative purpose and an adjacent supporting non-hold beat, without compulsory limb motion. Every opening/body/ending shot and unchanged principal-picture state must still last at most three seconds. Crops, subtitle updates, micro-zooms, decorative effects or empty cuts do not restart state dwell; no author-declared exceptional hold grants longer shots. Fit events/actual voice and readable results within the limit; an entirely static film or camera-only slideshow still fails. This scoped exception leaves A and B1–B3 unchanged. Otherwise track meaningful event times and inspect the gaps, including shot beginnings/endings. Main-subject acting, genuine prop interaction, a developing diagram, substantive information reveals or relevant moving footage may sustain a longer shot. Allow settling/reading time and keep a clear focal point; this is not a maximum shot length or compulsory hard cuts every 2.5 seconds.
- Subtitle turnover, whole-frame zoom/pan/fade, decorative noise/pulses, repeated unrelated nods or a tiny moving corner do not rescue a frozen principal picture. Movement must communicate the current idea and be legible at playback size. Do not add distracting motion just to beat a detector.
- Do not repeat compositions to fill time. Review distant sections as well as neighbors for the same pose/layout with replacement text. Change the focal subject, viewpoint, action or information relationship where the meaning changes. Reuse valid assets for continuity and motivated callbacks; variety is not a reason to regenerate everything.
- Vary entry order by meaning: sometimes a complete scene, sometimes text/prop/character first. Connect useful actions/props across cuts. Avoid the repeated title → icon → underline build or constant restless transitions.

For **B3**, execute the [semantic lines and purposeful motion gate](b3-semantic-motion.md): plan caption-linked events, bind connector endpoints/directions to actual objects, classify every relevant mark and exclude emphasis/decoration or ordinary CRT texture from principal-motion credit. Independently inspect every event/graphic and visible-inventory completeness in each continuous encoded shot. Neither a nonempty reason, moving pixels nor geometrically matching declared anchors proves correct meaning; false targets/directions and unrelated filler remain blockers in limited mode too. Preserve the existing 2.5-second gate without adding arbitrary gestures to satisfy it.

## Clear screenshots and content-matched graphics

Use genuine, sharp screenshots/recordings when they explain the point. Crop/enlarge the relevant area and check readability in the **final encoded frame**, including small-view playback; upscaling blur is not a repair. Allow enough reading time through purposeful main-picture progression. Apply the selected style's framing without covering key evidence, faces, hands or captions. A diagram/animated crop must not masquerade as actual screen recording.

Match audience language; prefer genuine English UI/source for English videos. If unavailable, use accurate labeled illustration/diagrams or relevant real moving samples rather than a prolonged unreadable foreign-language screenshot. Needed original-language quotations may remain with separate translation. Never repaint translated copy over a capture and present it as genuine UI. Verify names, dates, links and displayed figures against cited sources/capture dates; another creator's example is not proof they used this Skill.

Match icons, platform/company marks, person sketches, hand-drawn objects and charts to the actual entity/action being discussed. Use a coherent, licensed icon family or original drawings with enough detail to be recognizable. Avoid generic stand-ins for essential objects, unrelated logo piles and decorative dashboards. Data charts require supported data; label conceptual illustrations honestly. Keep one dominant idea/action and enough negative space: useful detail, not clutter.

## Natural captions and semantic sound

Narration, synchronized subtitles and meaningful action SFX are required for all B previews/films. Apply the selected B style's caption font, size, placement, safe area and emphasis; do not impose one universal visual design. Screen emphasis and captions must remain readable without competing or covering essential content.

B4 additionally applies its [typography acceptance contract](b4-typography-acceptance.md) to opening/rule/warning/choice text and semantic captions in every language: story-adapted hierarchy/material finish, visible local micro-animation, readable settling and independent actual-MP4 review. Its default opening remains `b4-hook-v1`; ordinary captions may stay restrained. Do not transfer these B4 treatments or runtime gates to another style.

B4 picture animation is a separate mandatory track under its [picture contract](b4-picture-animation.md). Plan before/intermediate/after states and assets before generation, test the hardest action locally, then execute bounded batches. A capable Codex/Claude Code workflow authors and renders those states without waiting for user reminders. Neither animated text, a completed ledger, a GIF filename nor a successful encode establishes body-picture animation or sample-comparable quality. A tested unresolved tool limit must remain an explicit limited/unreviewed result, without inventing paid authorization or an equivalent pass.

B1–B4 and B6 styles, languages, previews and films use **semantic subtitles with at most one visible line at a time**. B5 alone allows its approved upper-right block of up to three semantic lines; apply the meaning, actual-voice alignment, word coverage, safe-area and readability checks below without imposing the B1–B4 line count. Automatic grouping is a **draft**, not semantic acceptance. Review every cue boundary against meaning and actual speech pauses. Preserve all spoken words and order; keep noun phrases, verbs with necessary objects, modifiers, names, numbers/units and phrasal verbs together when readable. Avoid stranded prepositions/determiners or severing tight grammatical groups solely to meet a fixed word/character count or duration. A cue may be a complete sentence or a coherent clause/phrase. If it is too wide, choose a semantic break within the selected style's line limit or successive cues, not mechanically smaller/squeezed type. After regrouping, each cue starts at its own first aligned spoken word. Recheck punctuation, gaps/overlap, end times and reading time against the original audio, and neighboring cues for broken meaning. Check both burned-in captions and sidecars; verify the selected line limit, safe areas and readable type in the final encoded frame and intended playback size. No newline alone is not proof of single-line rendering. The bundled grouping command does not perform this semantic review automatically.

Keep the selected native named voice fixed without added voice/style descriptions by default. For B4 only, also execute its required [audio contract](b4-audio-direction.md): fixed Brian narrator, automatically cast plot-needed roles with supported acting direction, dry-source performance checks before scene processing, and source/material/causality checks for SFX. This does not retune other B styles or revoice accepted media. Use original/licensed, action-specific sounds at the relevant instant, not a pop on every word. Listen for intelligible voice, balanced effects/music and clipping. Do not duplicate SFX in an already mixed master. Music is optional and style-specific, not A's mandatory piano.

Apply the required [B sound system](autopilot-sound-system.md) for **every genre**. Check the project-local scene/beat sound script covers the exact text from opening through ending: each scene's bed, action material/distance/sync, emotional job, cue priorities and the rights manifest. Implied sounds must not invent plot events. A continuing bed need not restart every sentence; AUDIO CUT must state which layers stop/remain and the return cue. Keep voice recommendations separate from applied settings; verify any explicit tuning override and supported controls without changing accepted recordings. Adapt to genre/style; use the suspense/apocalypse module only when relevant. Inspect material/weight, near/far/occlusion, emotional progression, motivated silence and hit hierarchy—not effect count. Three functional layers need not play simultaneously. Check the encoded mix for voice masking, harsh peaks, loop/join artifacts, mono/small-speaker translation and repeated-cue fatigue. Record measured loudness/true peak and unavailable listening checks honestly; verify asset-specific use rights before final delivery.

## Whole-film delivery gate

Apply the **same detail standard throughout the entire film**, not just the first 30 seconds or approved sample. Inspect every shot/ledger row, then review the actual final picture and sound from beginning to end:

B1/B2/B3 additionally complete `accept-visual` using the final film's own independently reviewed evidence under the [reference-effect protocol](b1-b2-b3-reference-acceptance.md#runtime-evidence-and-commands). The gate binds actual files, scope and review records; truthful playback, listening and aesthetic comparison remain a reviewer responsibility. `best_effort_limited` never becomes a sample-equivalent claim.

B4 additionally completes independent `accept-picture` and `accept-text` on the final MP4's own evidence. Review every shot and every text moment separately, complete source playback and cross-section repetition/continuity; a passing preview never approves unseen later sections. Keep picture/text/audio/user results distinct and repair only failed ranges.

B6 additionally completes its schema-v3 [motion review](b6-motion-review.md) with all 35 detailed decisions, actual candidate/reference playback, every active identity/view/contact, native encoded detail and full-range original-speed sound. Preserve predecessor repair history; current fingerprints and rendered corrections are required. A successful check validates evidence completeness, not aesthetics. Pending/failed findings keep authorized local correction active; publication, new costs and extension still follow the current user authorization.

1. **Meaning and continuity:** actions/icons/evidence match narration; facts are supported; hook, body and ending connect. Body/end retain the opening's artwork detail, typography care, evidence sharpness, meaningful motion and sound treatment. No downgrade to low-detail static slides after an attractive hook.
2. **Picture and repetition:** inspect a chronological contact sheet, necessary encoded full-size frames and both sides of revised cuts. Check focal hierarchy, character/prop contact, safe areas, screenshot clarity/language and distant repetition against the selected style.
3. **Timing:** inspect the encoded main-picture region **excluding the subtitle band** for inactivity longer than 2.5 seconds, comparing suspect intervals to the semantic-event ledger at playback speed. For B4 verify the hard three-second maximum for every complete shot and every unchanged principal-picture state across cuts, alongside its per-shot before/intermediate/after/hold evidence and independent actual-MP4 picture review. A shortened preview cannot conceal an overlong source shot. Verify short motivated holds' narrative/readability purpose and actual adjacent supporting animated beats; crops, subtitle updates, micro-zooms and decoration do not reset state dwell. A pixel-freeze detector is only an aid: camera drift, noise or a tiny inset can hide a frozen subject. Movement alone does not establish meaning or readability.
4. **Captions and audio:** compare the entire cue sequence to approved text/aligned words, check semantic boundaries and the selected style's line count/fit in encoded frames and subtitle sidecars, then watch/listen to the actual timing and mix. Successful encoding, frame counts, waveform/loudness checks and static sheets do not prove a good hook, natural phrasing, refined animation or intelligibility. Record unavailable playback/listening as missing review and seek user review rather than claiming it happened. B1–B3 runtime caption/motion/audio checks and B5's [production manifest](b5-production-gate.md) catch technical omissions; they do not automatically enforce these creative gates.
5. **Preservation and handoff:** record gate results and any failing timestamps, fix only affected shots/cues, and retain accepted script/audio/assets by fingerprint. Save the MP4, subtitle sidecars, ledger, checkpoint and QA results. Sample approval, remaining-production authorization, costs and publication remain governed by [Autopilot](autopilot.md); this quality checklist grants no new paid calls, uploads, showcase replacement or GitHub publication.
