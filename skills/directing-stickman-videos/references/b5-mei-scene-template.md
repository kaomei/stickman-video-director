# Mei scene template for a new B5 story

This is a reusable adaptation of the [verified original prompt](b5-mei-original-prompt.md), not another historical user message. **Before current-story composition on first use or any new B5 story, read the [latest user-approved worked instance](b5-mei-filled-example.md), study its [actual approved film](../assets/b5/local-baselines/mei-event-relay/approved-sample.mp4), and read the [worked takeover mechanisms and source](../examples/b5-mei-event-relay/README.md).** Follow the [default reference use](b5-first-preview.md#read-the-actual-reference-once), noting honest observed-to-current-script transfers briefly in the scene plan; a separate hash-bound study report belongs to optional strict QA. These resources are required; the blank template is not a substitute. Fill the template in the production project before implementation. Use the four scene fields and successive main-visual action list; do not substitute a six-phase physical checklist for the whole scene.

```text
GOAL
Turn [approved script] into a high-energy B5 visual-event sequence for
[native ratio, duration]. Transfer the latest approved worked relay's successive
main visuals, scale contrast, decisive movement and readable handoffs. Preserve
CrazyMei + Spider's drawing, bold silhouettes, timing and layered aftermath.

REFERENCE TRANSFER (from the actual approved worked film)
- Observed lead visual → decisive change → read/result → next lead visual:
  [actual timestamps and visible mechanism, not inferred from source code].
- Current-script transfers: [which mechanisms/scale/timing relationships apply,
  and how this script uses its own visual representations rather than old props].
- For each current_story_sequence event, record referent [the subject/relation],
  preserved_identity_cues [visible shape/context/contact ownership retained], and
  visible_information_gain [the actual state, order or uncertainty made visible].
  These are planned claims to test in pictures, not evidence of a successful render.
- Study modality and limits: [playback / decoded frames; actual unperformed checks].

CRITICAL ANIMATION DIRECTIVES
- Keep [approved presenter/identity], [approved voice/mix] and actual word timing.
- Warm paper, decisive dark contours, restrained red; native full stage with
  existing caption/presenter safe areas. No static UI-container slides.
- Each new meaning chooses a specific visual verb, metaphor or transformation.
  Let one main visual lead into the next. Objects, words and graphics can lead;
  do not make every beat a figure acting in the same room. For each handoff, name
  leading image before → leading image after → spoken meaning gained. Distinguish
  that compositional handoff from the semantic action or state change inside it:
  a new main image entering does not by itself show the spoken action. Repeating
  a pose/action under another label needs a new visible explanatory result.
  A camera-size change alone is local coverage, not a new relay event: each
  conceptual handoff needs a visibly new lead, representation or relationship.
- MAIN VISUAL means the pictured lead: its silhouette, scale, contrast and placement
  take precedence at delivery-size thumbnails. At takeover, exit, replace or demote
  the former main group and draw the new lead independently. A named JSON lead or
  small gesture inside a still-dominant whole-actor scene does not implement this.
- A magnification, crop or substitution meant to depict the same subject must
  preserve its recognizable referent and meaningful ownership/support/contact. Keep
  distinguishing contours and enough connecting context in the resulting image;
  a label cannot rescue a silhouette that depicts another object or relation.
  Keep those cues compact. Part-only and graphic scenes may lead without the
  whole actor/tableau; preserve ordinary Mei geometry wherever a figure appears.
- Compose the full requested duration, marking actual speech end. Let the current
  meaning choose the ending: continue its supported action, consequence and delayed
  tails, then give the result a purposeful read, or hand off to the next meaning
  when that meaning belongs in the requested excerpt. Preserve the original speech
  speed. Unused seconds are not a reason to collect prior shots into side-by-side
  panels, an order chain or a summary. Use comparison/order only when that relation
  itself explains the current words; it is not the default outro. Plan the last
  event's pacing across the available time without idle padding or invented acts;
  no event quota.
- An observation/investigation beat makes the relevant clue visible through a
  subject change, detail or comparison. Show what the current words invite us to
  notice, beyond merely repeating already-known location endpoints with arrows.
  A time-order graphic can itself explain a meaningful relation; it cannot stand
  in for every promised behavioral observation. Preserve uncertainty about causes
  and motives. Preparation, contact and settling of an already named action are
  legitimate visual development; explicitly supplied source examples can also be
  illustrated while retaining their hypothetical/example framing. Do not fabricate
  new acts or facts solely to manufacture richness.
- Give the leading entrance/change a short decisive motion, readable result,
  and an explicit exit/handoff. Overlap only tails that remain legible.
- Use punch-ins/impact accents selectively on the stage. Captions/presenter
  stay in screen space; reactive host motion supports the stage.
- Ordinary stickmen use the source Mei drawing proportions. Named comic
  deformation has bounded entry/extreme/recovery. Do not stretch normal limbs.
- Use the installed executable B5 art kit for source contours, complete poses,
  whole cartoon animals, safe framing and evaluated motion endpoints. Abstract
  comic motion and graphic replacement are ordinary choices; animal joint
  simulation is optional, not a realism requirement. Add current-story drawings
  only where needed; choose the representation from the spoken meaning.
- Before tweening, prepare the current events' reusable object drawings, visible
  states and key poses. Resolve anatomy, shared contact/support and layer ownership
  in those drawings first; drawing more icons is not a substitute for a relay.
  Keep this within existing scene authoring, without an extra approval/report round.
- Preserve factual meaning/uncertainty. Reuse authorized media; do not infer
  new voice, paid generation, music or publication permission from this template.

OPTIMIZED SCENE BREAKDOWN

Scene [N]: [one visual premise] ([actual voice-aligned start–end])
- Voiceover: "[exact approved words]"
- On-Screen Text: "[brief editorial phrase or none]"
  [entrance/emphasis/exit, separate from full semantic captions]
- Animation Action:
  1. At [word/phrase and actual time], [MAIN VISUAL A] [specific verb/path].
     Before → decisive change/result: [...]. Read [what, how long, why].
     Pictured lead/hierarchy: [...]. Subordinate ownership/context only: [...].
  2. At [next semantic trigger], [MAIN VISUAL B] takes over by [wipe/transform/
     replacement/reframe/contact...]. Relation to A: [meaning].
     Leading motion: [endpoint/duration/curve]. Tail: [separate delay/exit].
  3. At [...], [MAIN VISUAL C] [...]. End state/handoff: [...].
  [Use as many meaningful events as this scene needs; no fixed count/quota.
   For actual physical events, add the six contact phases in an action card.
   For graphic events, describe real visual before/after samples, not fake force.]
- SFX: [event ID + actual material/entrance sound] → [next event sound]
       → [tail/resolution or deliberate silence].
  [Reuse actual approved sources; align contacts to actual transient offsets.]

[Repeat Scene fields for the next meaning.]

IMPLEMENTATION AND INTERNAL REVIEW
- Plan main visual before → result → next main visual at delivery size.
  Record event timing, actions and handoffs in this scene plan; render
  deterministically from time. A separate plan-lock is optional strict QA.
- Keep the pictured referent, ownership/contact and visible claim readable.
  An ambiguous sketch may need a quick thumbnail check before implementation;
  an independent whole-storyboard rehearsal/report is not a default prerequisite.
- Author one meaningful lead visual after another. Similar desk poses or
  decorative icons around an idle figure need re-direction.
- Render and encode an internal candidate, then review it once at normal speed
  with sound against the latest approved relay and CrazyMei/Spider standard.
  Check lead-image handoffs, scale/timing contrast, anatomy/contact, graphic
  hierarchy and semantic caption/audio timing during that same pass.
- Inspect suspect frames only when needed. Repair an observed defect locally,
  then recheck the affected interval and neighboring handoff/caption transition.
  Do not automatically repeat unrelated whole-image or storyboard audits.
- Record a short truthful QA note and any unavailable playback/listening.
  Template presence and technical checks do not certify effect or user acceptance.
- Blind reads, seals, independent formal reports and complete evidence-bound
  plans are optional strict QA; follow the formal acceptance guide only when
  that workflow is explicitly selected.
```

The original five scenes and their exact jokes are an archive, not nouns to swap blindly. For example, a causal explanation may use a sudden signal → gaze magnification → brief physical collision → spill freeze → time-order transformation. The semantic handoff is the transferable idea; the current script and voice choose the actual sequence and pace.
