## Actual output

| Gate | Required actual-output evidence / reject conditions |
|---|---|
| Identity and connections | Check all shots, turns, bends, contacts and edit joins in the encoded film. Neutral-white circular face, two fine curved tufts, thin uniform trunk/limbs and consistent head size must survive. Reject visible head/neck gaps, detached hands/limbs, extra joints/fingers, persistent yellow face, thicker hair clumps, filled/triangular chest, unexpected costume or bow tie. Inspect suspect frames around the failure as well as normal-speed visibility. |
| Drawing and cross-scene stability | Compare each location with the kitchen primary reference and relevant clean scene stills at a similar head size: confident visible pen contours, inked seams/joints/worn edges, flat matte paint with sparse brush/stipple texture, broad simple tone regions, narrow drawn contact shadows and quiet period-stage depth. Reject smoothly wrapped 3D shading, glossy furniture, ambient occlusion, photoreal surfaces, decorative clutter or a generic sepia/monochrome filter replacing the painted scenery. A matching palette, an attached image or an unchanged fixed prompt alone is insufficient. New settings require their own review. |
| Acting, contact and story meaning | Watch the full performance at normal speed. Each main beat has a visible cause, readable action and consequence/reaction. Check weight, steps, grip/release, collisions and stable prop states. Reject idle poses disguised by camera moves, arbitrary limb flailing, foot sliding, floating props or elastic movement that breaks body continuity. Rich acting does not mean motion at every instant; a brief motivated reaction hold may be useful. |
| Camera, editing and rhythm | Follow the shared [action and camera continuity checks](a-production-and-review.md#action-and-camera-continuity). Watch the final sequence with its sound, including each insert's entry and return. Match action phase, body/prop state, screen direction, apparent head size and camera momentum; reject repeated action starts, unmotivated framing resets and ghosted anatomy used to hide a mismatch. Framing and shot changes serve setup/reveal/reaction/payoff. For music briefs, compare accents and phrase changes with actions/cuts. Reject repetitive drifting shots, unexplained jumps, excessive empty holds or frantic cuts that prevent reading the gag. Use the sample's level of performance, not its exact shot count, cut offset or story. |
| Text, when requested | Check every caption at final display size: semantic phrasing and correct timing, no omissions/repetition/overlap, readable duration, contrast and safe area, expressive but controlled animation, and no collision with key action. Lyrics are optional and sample-specific. No text requested means an explicit `not_applicable`; it does not mean adding sample lyrics by default. |
| Final sound and file | Decode the complete final file and verify dimensions, ratio, duration, streams and start/end. Then actually watch/listen to the full encoded mix at normal speed: complete natural speech, native mouth sync, continuous music phrases, correctly mapped action effects, balanced ambience, clean joins/tails and no unintended clipping, silence, doubled model audio, separation artifacts or abrupt cutoffs. Meter values, correlation and a silent contact sheet do not establish a listening pass; unavailable listening capability is `not_checked`. Intentional silence records the approved creative choice. |

## Review record and repair loop

Record output path and SHA-256 when a file is available; for an authorized browser-only test record the result URL/job ID and explicitly unavailable file hash without downloading against the user's instruction. Record requested ratio/settings, exact submitted prompt and prompt-lock hashes, actual reference inputs/roles (or disclosed text-only diagnostic), reviewed intervals, comparison stills, observed defects, reviewer/method and date. Full normal-speed viewing and final encoded listening must be explicit separate records; sampled frames are labelled sampled, never full playback. If the current tool cannot play/hear continuously, retain `not_checked`, state the limitation and provide the smallest review handoff. Do not invent a pass or call user approval inferred from silence.

Correct confirmed failures locally when feasible; otherwise revise only the offending scene/action within existing generation authorization. Preserve accepted audio and shots. Recheck the affected intervals and adjacent cuts, and perform a final complete viewing/listening of the changed assembled film before finished acceptance. A fresh project, native ratio or language starts with fresh evidence, not copied passes. Mechanical hash/link/media checks validate assets and packaging only.
