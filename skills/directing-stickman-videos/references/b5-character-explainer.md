# B5 · 角色解说风 / Character Explainer

**Turn your favorite avatar into a stickman star.** Former local B6 / Animated Meme is now B5. This is a route in the existing Skill, not another installation. Select it explicitly; do not alias it to B1 or B4.

## Input and output

Accept a topic/script and a chosen or uploaded presenter. Produce a recognizable abstract cartoon head on a simple stick body, speaking at bottom right; the left stage tells the idea through energetic anonymous stickmen, oversized objects, successive visual metaphors, graphic transformations and causal physical comedy. Warm paper, dark ink, restrained red accents, large editorial titles and voice-aligned captions form one composition. The main stage mixes physical comedy with bold story-specific graphic events; a realistic talking head does not replace it.

**Keep the two roles separate.** The [image/SVG decision](b5-avatar-motion.md#when-to-generate-an-image-when-to-code) applies only to the bottom-right presenter: reuse accepted assets first; new real people or complex anime/cartoon/IP identities require the current Agent's available image tool or accepted supplied raster art, while simple original geometric characters may use SVG. Codex is not required. With no image tool, configure/import an image instead of substituting a code-drawn face. Both new routes require static approval before a speaking test. Any left-stage figures are anonymous code-animated stickmen; some main visual events may be graphic-only; choosing a presenter does not recast those actors.

Read [avatar and motion direction](b5-avatar-motion.md), the mandatory [left-stage action contract](b5-left-stage-motion.md), [visual acceptance protocol](b5-visual-acceptance.md), [sound and acceptance](b5-sound-qa.md), the [common B sound system](autopilot-sound-system.md) and the bundled [provenance](../assets/b5/CREDITS.md) before production. Inspect the complete [approved sample](../assets/b5/approved-sample.mp4), then the [true-frame contact sheet](../assets/b5/reference/contact-sheet.jpg) and code, not only thumbnails. These assets are implementation references, not authorization to reuse a character, person's likeness, voice or account mark in any context.

**CrazyMei and Spider are the B5 visual/action quality standards for both landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16.** Inspect the [49.2s CrazyMei complete film](../assets/b5/local-baselines/crazymei/approved-sample.mp4) and [22.8s Spider complete film](../assets/b5/approved-sample.mp4), then their useful motion intervals and implementation. Read the [first-preview execution recipe](b5-first-preview.md) before authoring. The [baseline registry](b5-accepted-baselines.md) separates these two primary targets from Jensen V13, which remains a historical regression/example only. Neither sample's exact gags, voice, account or presenter automatically belong in a new story.

## Mandatory latest-effect study

On first B5 invocation and before every new-story plan, read the [latest user-approved filled Mei example](b5-mei-filled-example.md) in full and visually study its [exact approved relay film](../assets/b5/local-baselines/mei-event-relay/approved-sample.mp4). They are required alongside the original Mei prompt and the CrazyMei/Spider drawing/action masters. Do not defer them as optional examples or assume the blank template includes their decisions. Follow [the default reference study](b5-first-preview.md#read-the-actual-reference-once): note the useful lead-visual, timing and handoff mechanisms briefly in the current Mei scene plan. Normal previews need no separate structured study report or plan-lock. Preserve the distinction between actual playback, decoded-frame observations and generated evidence; hashes and boards do not prove viewing.

Before current-story composition, also read the mandatory [worked takeover mechanisms and source](../examples/b5-mei-event-relay/README.md) beside the actual film frames. Learn how the former main group clears or becomes subordinate and an independently composed lead takes over; the source's gag is not a new-story default. In the actual picture, the current lead must dominate through silhouette, scale and hierarchy. Keep whole-actor context only where needed for ownership; compact fragments can preserve meaning without displacing part/object/graphic leads. [Let the current meaning choose the ending](b5-left-stage-motion.md#end-on-the-current-meaning), including supported action/result development after speech; never force a side-by-side recap or summary to fill time.

## Left-stage planning gate

Before coding a new story, read the [verified original prompt](b5-mei-original-prompt.md), fill the [Mei scene template](b5-mei-scene-template.md), then apply [directing structure](b5-crazymei-directing-structure.md) and [action cards](b5-left-stage-motion.md#action-cards-before-code) against actual approved voice timing. Plan successive major visual events, meaningful metaphors, transformations, graphic paths and physical gags. Name the leading change, read interval, exit/handoff and separately delayed tails. Add force/contact/whole-body phases only to physical events. Do not default to one continuous domestic task or decorate an idle figure with noun icons. Choose story-specific visual mechanisms without inventing factual evidence or events.

Before tweening, [prepare the current events' objects, visible states and key contact poses](b5-left-stage-motion.md#draw-needed-states-before-tweening). Reuse suitable approved artwork and inspect ambiguous silhouettes during authoring; compact ownership cues allow parts/objects/graphics to lead without the whole actor/tableau. This does not add an approval stage. Independent storyboard rehearsal and plan-lock belong only to [optional strict QA](b5-first-preview.md#optional-strict-qa).

The neutral starter is not an automatic choreography engine. A new script needs its own visual event relay and actual candidate review, not just replacement text and props. Wherever figures or physical actions appear, add staged root/body motion and natural task-driven arm poses. Static raised arms across unrelated beats, unsupported reaches and repeated generic bursts fail. Technical render/IK/pixel checks cannot approve the animation. Every new encode stays pending until the actual review required by the selected preview route is performed; do not promote it to an accepted motion baseline without the user's actual review. Normal previews use the existing single-pass review, not independent motion-strip/report rounds.

## Proven capability and boundaries

- Primary reference composites: user-approved **CrazyMei 49.2s + Spider 22.8s**, both 1920×1080/30fps with exact accepted audio. They set the same quality bar in both ratios. Jensen V13 is retained for historical regression, not as the current B5 target. Use the [baseline matrix and review gates](b5-accepted-baselines.md), not a memorized prop sequence.
- The installed B5 code example and frame renderer are self-contained. The agent must author new scenes, prop interactions, timing, semantic caption groups, mouth envelope and an authorized final audio mix for each new script. The helper does not generate scripts, rig arbitrary uploaded images or buy voice/SFX services.
- B5 must support native landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16, including a requested pair, under the [common dual-format gate](autopilot-production-qa.md#native-landscape-and-portrait). The complete primary reference media are landscape; the same CrazyMei + Spider quality standard applies to portrait. A portrait film still needs its own observed acceptance. A 9:16 version needs native re-layout of stage, captions and presenter plus a fresh preview; do not crop or squeeze the landscape output and claim a validated vertical template. Preserve accepted identity and original-speed narration rather than generating them again just for a ratio change.
- New identity: select and record its image/SVG route, first approve the static head/body design, lock the accepted assets, then make a roughly five-second speaking test, then the story preview. Existing approved identity and audio are reused unchanged. Static approval alone is not approval of a whole new film or permission for paid generation/publication.

## Installed execution

For first use or a new story, follow the [default first-preview recipe](b5-first-preview.md): actual approved reference study → current Mei scene plan → author → validate/render/encode internal candidate → one normal-speed playback review with sound → necessary local repair → requested preview. Technical diagnostics do not certify the effect. Strict evidence-bound QA is a separate opt-in route.

From the installed Skill directory, run `npm ci` and `node scripts/autopilot.mjs doctor`. B5 additionally uses Sharp. FFmpeg/FFprobe and Node 20+ are required. Project files always live outside the installed Skill.

```sh
node scripts/autopilot.mjs init /absolute/new-project --style B5
node scripts/character-explainer.mjs check
```

Default B5 `init` exposes the mandatory latest filled example, approved relay film and reference-study resources. It copies the shared renderer, identity/text/audio infrastructure and reference assets, then installs the neutral [new-story stage API](../examples/b5-mei-event-relay/new-story-stage.cjs) as `scene/stage.cjs`. The starter deliberately throws until the current narration's events are authored; it cannot silently render historical Spider choreography. Copied script/timing/glyph/audio data remain sample material to replace or reconfigure for the current story. `check` verifies the installed historical baseline hashes, video/audio packets, caption word coverage and fixed-length arm geometry; it does not render or approve the neutral starter. After planning and authorship, use `frames` with the actual story's sample times or complete coverage. A plan checkpoint is optional strict QA. It executes local code only with explicit `--trusted-code`; inspect it before granting that flag. It refuses existing output directories and records input fingerprints.

The exact historical Spider scene is an explicit inspection/fidelity option: `node scripts/character-explainer.mjs init /absolute/reference-project --reference-scaffold`, or `initB5(project, {referenceScaffold: true})` in code. It is not the new-story default. Default init sets the project's `approved-reference.mp4` to the latest approved relay; the explicit historical scaffold pairs that path with its matching Spider film. Both return the mandatory latest-relay resources, so inspecting historical code does not change the new-story effect target. Validate the additional local baseline with `node --test tests/b5-local-baseline.test.mjs`; never edit the locked baseline in place. Neither passing test suite replaces story-specific choreography, listening or user review.

For a new story, replace the sample script/timings/glyph tiles, scenes, identity and sound plan as required, and provide the [production manifest](b5-production-gate.md). Only the byte-identical historical scene bypasses that new-story gate. **No account watermark or test label by default**: leave `account` empty. Add branding only when the user explicitly requests their own mark; never copy a reference creator's handle. The immutable approved media may show historical example branding, which is not a style requirement. The bundled glyph atlas contains only this sample's words; rebuild tiles for new text rather than substituting blank or overflowing text. Recompute `speech-levels.json` from the approved **dry speech**, not BGM/SFX; its amplitude/word-onset mouth animation is a heuristic, not phoneme-accurate lip sync. Adapt expressions/gestures without stretching the accepted face.

Fonts, text effects and the video language are customizable: record an explicit user choice or a necessary language font fallback with the [scoped typography override](b5-production-gate.md#font-text-effect-and-language-customization), update the actual renderer/glyph tiles, and review the result. **Every language needs animated semantic captions**: actual-voice progressive reveal, meaningful emphasis and a readable hold, with language-correct shaping. Group at actual pauses and complete semantic dependencies; do not clear a preposition or subordinate introducer away from its required continuation. Preserve modifiers, verb–object relationships and phrasal verbs. Static whole sentences, mechanically split characters/words and a font-only substitution do not satisfy this gate. In the actual candidate playback, inspect caption entrance/emphasis/hold, adjacent group replacements and audio alignment; inspect suspect boundary frames only as needed. Masked stage images and plan text cannot establish caption quality. The [formal visual protocol](b5-visual-acceptance.md#every-caption-group-must-pass-in-every-language) is optional strict QA. Keep the default treatment otherwise; an override does not waive sound, local-picture motion or visual acceptance.

Sample voice credits are not requirements: the Spider-Man example uses **The Pharaoh 3** through the owner's selected service; the Jensen example uses **The Pharaoh 3** through **AI33**; Crazy Mei B5.2 uses **Claire**. Do not infer an unrecorded provider from a voice name. Use the user's selected available route, help configure it if needed, and preserve accepted audio. These credits do not authorize paid generation or force another Agent to use the same platform.

When production of the current film is authorized, author the current narration's scene plan, native layout, actions, semantic captions and approved mix, then create the internal candidate:

```sh
node scripts/character-explainer.mjs validate /absolute/new-project
node scripts/character-explainer.mjs frames /absolute/new-project --output /absolute/new-full-frames --all --trusted-code
node scripts/character-explainer.mjs encode /absolute/new-project --frames-dir /absolute/new-full-frames --audio /absolute/approved-final-mix.wav --output /absolute/new-candidate
```

`encode` verifies current frame/project fingerprints and approved audio/duration, fully decodes the result, and writes the MP4 plus `candidate.json` with review pending. It does not create a visual pass or user approval. Optional `--approval-note` records truthful provenance only. Outputs remain outside the project and refuse overwrites; no command mixes audio or grants paid calls/publication authority.

Inspect the actual encoded candidate once at normal speed with sound, comparing the lead-visual relay, drawing/proportions/contact, native composition, caption rhythm and speech with the approved reference. Keep a compact actual-file review/scope note. Inspect suspect frames only as needed; repair and recheck affected intervals plus necessary neighboring action/caption transitions. Follow the [default review and delivery scope](b5-first-preview.md#review-the-actual-candidate-once); do not start repeated full-image review rounds or skill cold-start tests while the user is waiting for their preview.

Deliver the requested MP4 preview with its verified absolute containing-folder link, source/checkpoint and truthful QA scope. Record the user's actual response separately. If continuous viewing/listening is unavailable, identify that limitation and leave those checks unverified; the requested preview can be returned with the limitation, without an effect-matched, AV-pass or formal-acceptance claim. Never publish the user's project, portrait or private audio without separate authorization.

### Optional strict formal QA

For explicit strict independent QA or a stated evidence-bound delivery contract, use the [formal protocol and detailed recipe](b5-visual-acceptance.md#strict-execution-recipe). It retains `reference-study`, independent storyboard rehearsal, pre-code `plan-lock`, `frames --plan-lock`, current-final-PNG first-read/seal/contextual checks, `review-pack`, `preview-ready` and `accept`. Strict PNG review flags opt `encode` into that exact-current-evidence gate. Its local-revision path can reuse only individually passed unchanged dependencies, never fail/unknown. These commands are not prerequisites for an ordinary preview; technical success and formal review still never imply user acceptance.
