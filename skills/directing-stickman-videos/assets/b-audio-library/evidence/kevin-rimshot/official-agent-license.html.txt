<OL><LI>uuid: This column represents the unique identifier for each music track. It ensures that each track can be easily referenced and distinguished from others in your catalog.</LI>

<LI>title: The title column contains the name of the music track, which is essential for users to identify and search for specific pieces.</LI>

<LI>filename: This column holds the filename of the music track, which can be used to locate and download the corresponding audio file. All audio files are located in the https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/mp3-royaltyfree/ directory.</LI>

<LI>length: The length column indicates the duration of the music track, typically displayed in the format "hh:mm:ss".</LI>

<LI>instruments: This column lists the musical instruments used in the composition of the track, providing users with an idea of the track's overall sound and style.</LI>

<LI>genre: The genre column categorizes each music track based on its musical style, making it easier for users to find tracks that fit their desired mood or theme. Key at https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/genre.json</LI>

<LI>bpm: This column displays the beats per minute (BPM) of the music track, which provides information about its tempo and pace.</LI>

<LI>description: The description column offers a brief overview of the music track, often including information about its mood, inspiration, and structure.</LI>

<LI>feel: (aka "mood") This column describes the emotional atmosphere or vibe of the music track, helping users choose tracks that match their project's tone.</LI>

<LI>uploaded: The uploaded column indicates the date when the music track was added to your catalog, providing users with an idea of the track's recency.</LI>

<LI>isrc: This column contains the International Standard Recording Code (ISRC), which is a unique identifier for individual recordings and helps with royalty tracking and rights management.</LI>

<LI>collection: The collection column groups related music tracks together, making it easier for users to discover and navigate through similar tracks. Key location: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/collections.json</LI>

<LI>sheetmusic: This column provides information about any available sheet music for the track, which can be useful for musicians or those looking to perform the piece.</LI>

<LI>video: The video column includes links to any associated videos, such as music videos or visualizations that accompany the track.</LI>

<LI>itunes: This column contains links to the track's iTunes page, where users can purchase or stream the music through Apple's platform.</LI>

<LI>wav: The wav column may include links to high-quality WAV files of the music track, offering users an alternative audio format for download. Extra charges may apply.</LI>

<LI>filmmusicURL: This column contains links to additional resources or information related to the music track, such as its use in film, television, or other media projects. Currently defunct.</LI>
