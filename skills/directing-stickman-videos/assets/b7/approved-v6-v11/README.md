Both validate their accepted **16:9** compositions. Another ratio needs deliberate recomposition and its own inspection. Brian is an accepted example voice, not the B7 default. Paths used a specifically approved 1.1x retime; Fast Forward uses native voice speed.

The reference movies contain finished mixed narration and music. No separate voice, music, model or soundtrack assets are supplied, and their inclusion is not a license to extract or reuse those elements in a new project. Select and clear a new story's sound independently. Preserve the linked music credits whenever distributing or publicly showcasing these reference films.

Historical silent excerpts remain one directory above for identity and contact study; the two complete films here are the newer action, ending and sound acceptance benchmarks. Acceptance and fingerprints are recorded in [the manifest](../reference-manifest.json). Technical checks preserve this evidence; they do not automatically approve a new film.
