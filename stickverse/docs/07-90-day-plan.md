# 07 · The 90-day plan (26 Oct 2026 → 31 Jan 2027)

You have a **98-day window** before the YPP entry bar doubles. This plan is built to use it:
get 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours while they are still the price of admission.

If you miss it, the plan still works — you just need 8,000 hours instead of 4,000. Do not let
the deadline make you publish garbage. A demonetised channel is worse than a slow one.

---

## The shape of the 98 days

| Phase | Dates | Goal | Weekly output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sprint 0** | 26 Oct – 1 Nov | Channel fully set up, 3 videos in the bank | 0 published |
| **Phase 1 · Foundation** | 2 Nov – 29 Nov | Find the format that works | 2 long + 12 Shorts |
| **Phase 2 · Iterate** | 30 Nov – 27 Dec | Double down on the winning format | 2 long + 16 Shorts |
| **Phase 3 · Compound** | 28 Dec – 24 Jan | Scale what works, kill what doesn't | 2–3 long + 20 Shorts |
| **Phase 4 · Convert** | 25 Jan – 31 Jan | Apply to YPP the day you qualify | 1 long + 7 Shorts |

Totals by 31 Jan: **~28 long-form videos, ~55 Shorts.**

## Phase 1 · Foundation (2 Nov – 29 Nov)

**Goal: learn what your audience responds to, not to go viral.**

| Week | Long-form (Fri 18:00) | Shorts | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Your Brain Lies To You* (psychology) | 6 | Packaging test: 3-word thumbnail |
| 2 | *The 3AM Habit* (human moments) | 6 | Hook test: first 3 seconds |
| 3 | *One Man vs an Empire* (true story) | 6 | Length test: 10 min vs 8 min |
| 4 | *They Pay You Less Than This* (money) | 6 | Topic test: which pillar holds retention |

**Weekly ritual (45 min, Sunday)**
1. CTR per video; AVD per video.
2. Which pillar got the best *subscribers per view*?
3. Which Short got the most "viewed vs swiped away"?
4. One change for next week.

**Gate at 29 Nov — do not skip this:**

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Any video has CTR > 5% | Keep that thumbnail style |
| Any long-form holds > 45% AVD | That topic shape is your format — repeat it |
| All videos under 500 views | The problem is packaging (title/thumb), not the algorithm. Test 3 new thumbnails this week |
| Subscribers < 50 | Your Shorts are not converting. Add a recurring character + a 2-second sign-off |
| Subscribers > 150 | You are ahead — hold the schedule, do not add volume |

## Phase 2 · Iterate (30 Nov – 27 Dec)

**Goal: make your best format repeatable.**

- Take the top-performing video from Phase 1 and make **three more in that exact shape**
  (different topics, same structure).
- Start a **series** with a name: *"Brain Lies #2"*, *"Empires #2"*. Series create binge
  sessions and suggested traffic.
- Double Shorts to **4 per week**, all with the same opening device.
- Add one **standalone Short** per week that is unrelated to any long-form — your discovery bet.
- Build your first **free lead magnet** (a 1-page "stickman script template" PDF) and link it in
  the description. That is how you start an email list for products later.

**Gate at 27 Dec**

| Metric | Target | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| Watch hours cumulative | 1,000+ | Publish one 15–20 min "deep dive" — long videos accumulate hours faster |
| Subscribers | 300+ | Cut your worst pillar; put all effort into the best two |
| CTR average | 4%+ | Redesign thumbnails with the `02-thumbnails-and-brand.md` rules |
| AVD average | 40%+ | Rewrite hooks; cut the first 15 seconds of every video |

## Phase 3 · Compound (28 Dec – 24 Jan)

**Goal: cross 1,000 subs and 4,000 hours.**

- **3 long-form per week is allowed here** if you can hold quality. If not, 2 + more Shorts.
- Re-release your best video with a new thumbnail and a new title (this is legal and normal —
  it is not re-uploading, it is re-packaging).
- Start the **collab week**: message 10 similar-size channels with a specific idea (a 60-second
  character crossover). Two yeses is a good week.
- Pitch **2 sponsors** for February. You are small, but you have a defined audience — that is
  what micro-sponsors buy.
- Make the first **digital product** (₹199–₹499 prompt + thumbnail pack). Sell to your comments
  and your Shorts bio link.

**Gate at 24 Jan**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Watch hours cumulative | 3,000+ |
| Subscribers | 700+ |
| Total published | 24 long-form, 45 Shorts |

If you are at 3,000 hours and 700 subs, you are **one good video away** from both thresholds.
That is the moment to publish your best idea, not to experiment.

## Phase 4 · Convert (25 Jan – 31 Jan)

- [ ] Check Studio → Earn: are you at 1,000 subs + 4,000 hours?
- [ ] If **yes**: apply to YPP immediately, before 31 Jan. Existing partners are not affected by
      the 2027 threshold change.
- [ ] If **no**: apply anyway the moment you cross, even in February — you will be measured
      against 8,000 hours, so plan a long-form-heavy February.
- [ ] Accept the updated YPP terms in Studio before **31 Jan 2027**.
- [ ] Set up mid-rolls on new long-form videos (manual placement beats automatic).
- [ ] Turn on Super Thanks and memberships if you are in the fan-funding tier.

## What "on track" looks like at 30 / 60 / 90 days

| | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Published long-form | 8 | 16 | 26 |
| Published Shorts | 24 | 44 | 60 |
| Subscribers | 120 | 400 | 1,000 |
| Watch hours | 350 | 1,300 | 4,000+ |
| Revenue | ₹0 | ₹0–₹2,000 (services) | ₹2,000–₹10,000 (services + maybe ads) |

If your numbers are half of this, you are still fine — most channels that last a year start
far below the curve. If you are at a *tenth* of this after 60 days, change the **format**, not
the schedule.

---

## The rules of the 90 days

1. **Never publish a video you have not watched twice.** Once as an editor, once as a viewer.
2. **Do not change niche before video 20.** The data is noise until then.
3. **Do not buy anything** until a bottleneck is measurable.
4. **Protect the Friday slot.** Consistency is a signal to both viewers and the algorithm.
5. **Ship the imperfect video.** A published 8/10 teaches you more than a perfect unpublished
   10/10.
6. **One experiment per week, logged.** That log becomes your channel's playbook.
