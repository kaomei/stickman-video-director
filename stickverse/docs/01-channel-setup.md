# 01 · Channel setup, start to monetised

This is the boring, high-leverage part. Do it once, properly, and you never touch it again.

Budget 90 minutes. Tick the boxes in order.

---

## Step 0 · What the old account means for you

You are using a Google account created around 2017. **Google does not penalise account age,
and an older account gets no reach bonus.** What actually matters:

| Thing | Why it matters | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Community Guidelines strikes on that account | A strike blocks YPP regardless of age | Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility. Must be clean. |
| Old videos on the channel | Off-topic uploads confuse the recommendation system and look like spam history | Unlist or delete anything unrelated to stickman content |
| 2-step verification | Mandatory for YPP and for advanced features | Turn it on now, before you upload anything |
| Recovery email / phone | You will lose the channel if you lose the login | Update both, and save backup codes offline |
| Payment history / AdSense | An old AdSense account with a disabled status is painful to fix | Check AdSense **now**, before you have 1,000 subs |

**Recommended: put StickVerse on a Brand Account.** It is a channel owned by your Google
account but with its own name, so:
- you can add a second manager (editor, VA, future you on another laptop),
- your personal name and photo never appear,
- you can hand the channel to a company later.

How: YouTube → profile picture → **Switch account → View all channels → Create a channel**
→ enter `StickVerse`. Do not use "use your name" — use a custom brand name.

> If your 2017 channel already has subscribers or history you want to keep, keep it and just
> rename it. Never make a second channel and re-upload the same videos — duplicate content
> across two channels you own is a policy problem.

## Step 1 · Claim the handle

`@stickverse` is probably taken. Handles are unique across all of YouTube. Try, in order:

1. `@stickverse`
2. `@stickversestories`
3. `@stickverseai`
4. `@thestickverse`
5. `@stickversehq`
6. `@stick.verse`

Rules: 3–30 characters, letters/numbers/underscore/period. Pick one and **keep it everywhere**
(Instagram, TikTok, X) even if you only squat those accounts — handles get squatted once a
channel grows.

## Step 2 · Upload the brand files

From `stickverse/brand/`:

| Slot | File | Spec | Upload where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile picture | `profile-800.png` | 800×800 PNG, renders as a 98 px circle | Studio → Customisation → Branding → Picture |
| Banner | `banner-2560x1440.png` | 2560×1440, under 6 MB | Branding → Banner image |
| Video watermark | `watermark-150.png` | 150×150 transparent PNG, under 1 MB | Branding → Video watermark (set display: **Entire video**) |
| End screen | `endscreen-1920x1080.png` | 1920×1080 | Not uploaded to branding — add it as the last 20 s clip in your editor |

Check the banner with `banner-safe-guide.png` first: the green box is the only area every
device shows. Everything outside it is decoration.

**Banner alternative:** YouTube now lets you upload an **animated banner** on some accounts.
Skip it for now — it distracts from the channel name.

## Step 3 · Channel copy (paste from `docs/09-templates-and-copy.md`)

1. **Description / About** — 2 lines of what the channel is, 1 line of schedule, 3 links,
   contact email. Include the phrase people would search: *"stickman stories explained"*.
2. **Channel trailer** — a 30–45 s cut of your best Shorts. Brand-new visitors decide in
   5 seconds; do not use a 2-minute "welcome to my channel".
3. **Featured sections** — create 3 shelves: *Start Here*, *Psychology*, *True Stories*.
4. **Links** — put your Instagram/TikTok and a contact email. No link shorteners.

## Step 4 · Settings that quietly decide your reach

Studio → Settings:

| Setting | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Upload defaults → Visibility | Private or Scheduled | Never publish accidentally |
| Upload defaults → Category | Education or People & Blogs | Best fit for explainers |
| Upload defaults → Comments | Hold potentially inappropriate for review | Protects a new channel from spam waves |
| Permissions → **Advanced features** | Apply as soon as it's offered | Needed for custom thumbnails, longer videos, live, A/B testing |
| Channel → Audience | **Not made for kids** | Stickman explainers are general audience. Marking kids' content kills comments and most ad revenue |
| Channel → Keywords | `stickman, animation, psychology, explainer` | Mild SEO help |
| Permissions → Managers | Add a second Google account | Account recovery |
| Monetisation | Link AdSense early | Approval can take weeks; do not wait until you qualify |

**Advanced features** are what let you upload custom thumbnails, use longer videos, live and
A/B testing. YouTube unlocks them once your account meets its own thresholds (identity/phone
verification, no active strikes, some channel activity). Check *Feature eligibility* weekly in
your first month — a clean account usually unlocks quickly.

## Step 5 · AdSense and tax, before you need them

Do this while you are still small, because it is the slowest step in the whole chain.

1. **Create/link AdSense** — Studio → Earn → Sign up. One AdSense account per person, ever.
   If your Google account already has one tied to an old blog, reuse it.
2. **Payee name must match your bank account exactly.** Mismatches are the #1 reason Indian
   creators wait months for the first payout.
3. **Address PIN** — Google mails a PIN to your address; it can take 3–4 weeks. Verify it as
   soon as it arrives.
4. **Tax info** — as an Indian creator you submit PAN and your tax residency. Google deducts
   tax at source on US-sourced earnings under the India–US treaty; the exact rate depends on
   the forms you submit. Keep every payout statement.
5. **GST / business setup** — ad revenue from a foreign platform is export of services. Once
   your turnover crosses the registration threshold you should be GST-registered and may need
   an LUT to avoid paying IGST on exports. **Confirm with a CA, not with a YouTube video.**
6. **Payout threshold** — AdSense pays monthly once you clear the threshold, via bank transfer.

> Rule of thumb: get AdSense approved at ~100 subscribers, not at 1,000. You will thank
> yourself on the day you qualify for YPP.

## Step 6 · Monetisation thresholds (the part that changed)

### Today (until 31 January 2027)

| Tier | Requirement | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Fan funding** | 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + (3,000 watch hours in 12 months **or** 3M Shorts views in 90 days) | Memberships, Super Thanks, Super Chat, Shopping |
| **Full YPP** | 1,000 subs + (**4,000 public watch hours in 12 months** *or* 10M Shorts views in 90 days) | Watch-page ads, Shorts ad revenue, Premium revenue |

Plus, always: 2-step verification, no active strikes, AdSense linked, YPP available in your
country (India is).

### From 1 February 2027 — for **new** applicants

| Requirement | New bar |
|---|---|
| Subscribers | 1,000 (unchanged) |
| Long-form path | **8,000** qualified watch hours in the last 365 days |
| Shorts path | **20 million** qualified Shorts views in 90 days |

- Creators already accepted into YPP are **not** affected.
- From the same date, earning from the **Shorts Creator Pool each month** requires maintaining
  **10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days** — even for existing partners. Missing it
  does not remove you from YPP or affect long-form earnings.
- New "active channel" definition: 1,000 watch hours/365 days, or 1M Shorts views/90 days, or
  2 long-form or 5 Shorts uploaded every 90 days.
- You must accept the updated YPP terms in Studio **by 31 January 2027**.

### What this means for you, concretely

**You have a 98-day window** (26 Oct 2026 → 31 Jan 2027) in which 4,000 hours still qualifies
you. That is the difference between needing ~60,000 long-form views and ~120,000.

Realistic plan:

- If you can hit **1,000 subs + 4,000 hours by 31 Jan 2027**, apply immediately.
- If you cannot, do not panic: the same content that earns 4,000 hours earns 8,000, it just
  takes longer. Your plan target should still be *apply in the cheap window*.

Shorts-only will not monetise a new channel in India. 10M (then 20M) Shorts views in 90 days
is a mass-market number; a 3-month-old storytelling channel will not reach it. Shorts are
your **discovery** engine — they feed subscribers into the long-form videos that produce
watch hours.

## Step 7 · Verify the channel is actually ready

Run this before your first upload:

- [ ] 2-step verification ON
- [ ] Brand Account created, handle claimed
- [ ] Profile picture, banner, watermark uploaded and previewed on **mobile**
- [ ] About section filled with schedule + contact email
- [ ] Advanced features: `Unlocked` (or "under review")
- [ ] Custom thumbnails: upload a test thumbnail to a private video
- [ ] Default upload settings: Private/Scheduled
- [ ] AdSense account created and linked
- [ ] Audience: not made for kids
- [ ] Comment moderation set to "hold potentially inappropriate"
- [ ] A folder on your drive named `StickVerse/` with subfolders `scripts`, `audio`, `art`,
      `projects`, `published`, `thumbnails`
- [ ] Your first three video ideas chosen from `topics/60-video-ideas.csv`
