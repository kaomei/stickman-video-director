# 10 · Upload checklist (one page, print it)

Keep this open while publishing. If a box is unticked, the video is not ready.

## Before you export

- [ ] I watched the whole thing at 1×, on a phone, sound on
- [ ] The first 5 seconds give a reason to stay *before* any intro or logo
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source on screen or in the description
- [ ] Every sentence sounds like something a person would say
- [ ] One idea, start to finish — nothing tacked on at the end
- [ ] Audio: voice clear at −16 LUFS, music at least 15 dB under it, no clipping
- [ ] No stretched figures, extra limbs, melting faces, or off-model characters
- [ ] Captions proofread (names, numbers, hard words)
- [ ] Nothing important in the bottom 12% (progress bar / UI)
- [ ] End screen: 20 s, two specific videos, subscribe

## Packaging

- [ ] Title is 40–60 characters and the promise is delivered by the video
- [ ] Thumbnail reads at 320×180 px (check `thumbnails/mobile-check/`)
- [ ] Thumbnail is 1280×720, under 2 MB, sRGB
- [ ] Thumbnail text = 6 words or fewer
- [ ] Title and thumbnail do not repeat each other — they complete each other
- [ ] I would click this if a stranger posted it

## Metadata

- [ ] Description: first line restates the promise with the searchable phrase
- [ ] Chapters / timestamps added
- [ ] 3 sources linked
- [ ] 10–15 tags, no keyword stuffing
- [ ] 3 hashtags including `#stickverse`
- [ ] Playlist assigned
- [ ] End screen + cards added
- [ ] Pinned comment written with a real question
- [ ] Audience: **not made for kids**
- [ ] Category: Education or People & Blogs
- [ ] AI disclosure: **not required** for clearly animated stickman content — only tick it if
      you use realistic synthetic people, real places, or AI music as the main focus
- [ ] Monetisation: mid-rolls placed manually (only after YPP)

## After publishing

- [ ] Reply to the first 20 comments within 60 minutes
- [ ] Post the Short teaser with a link in the pinned comment
- [ ] Share to Reels / TikTok / Facebook (watermark removed)
- [ ] Add the video to the relevant Community post
- [ ] Diary entry: what I tried this time, what I expect to happen

## 48 hours later

- [ ] CTR: ____ % (was it the packaging?)
- [ ] AVD: ____ % (was it the hook?)
- [ ] Biggest retention cliff at: ____ (fix this in the next video)
- [ ] Traffic sources: browse ____ / suggested ____ / search ____
- [ ] Subscribers gained: ____
- [ ] One decision for next week: ______________________________

---

### Red flags — do not publish if any of these is true

- It is a near-copy of a video you already made (inauthentic content)
- It is someone else's video with a new voice (reused content)
- It gives health, financial or legal advice through a fake persona (explicitly flagged)
- It uses music you did not license
- You have not watched the final file
- The title promises something the video does not deliver
