## 2 · Title formulas

| Pattern | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden mechanism | `Your brain [surprising verb] [thing] (here's why)` | *Your brain deletes most of today — here's what it keeps* |
| Contradiction | `Why [common belief] is backwards` | *Why resting makes you more tired* |
| Time + number | `The [number]-minute habit that costs you [loss]` | *The 20-minute habit that costs you 10 years* |
| Underdog | `The [person] who [refused/beat] [power]` | *The clerk who refused an empire* |
| Unfair truth | `Why [effort] stopped [reward]` | *Why working harder stopped paying* |
| Identity mirror | `You're not [label]. You're [real cause].` | *You're not lazy. You're under-stimulated.* |
| Explained | `[Thing] explained in [time]` | *Compound interest explained in 4 minutes* |

Rules: 40–60 characters · emotional word in the first 5 · no clickbait you cannot pay off ·
no more than one number.

## 6 · Shorts hook bank (first 1.5 seconds)

Pick one and go straight into it — no intro, no logo:

1. "Your brain is lying to you about [thing]."
2. "Nobody talks about the third option."
3. "This is why you feel tired after doing nothing."
4. "Everyone gets this wrong in their 20s."
5. "He had no army. He won anyway."
6. "Stop trying to be motivated."
7. "The most expensive thing you own is your attention."
8. "You've been told this your whole life. It's backwards."
9. "3AM again. Here's what your brain is actually doing."
10. "One sentence changed how I [verb]."

**Last frame:** the question, on screen, with the character looking at it. Not "follow for part 2"
— a *question* earns the comment; a demand earns the swipe.
