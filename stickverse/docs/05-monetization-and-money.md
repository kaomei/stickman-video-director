# 05 · Money: what it really pays, and how to earn before YPP

Read this before you spend a rupee on tools.

Three facts that change how you plan:

1. **Ads are the slowest, smallest income stream and the last one to switch on.**
2. **Indian views pay a fraction of US views, and Shorts pay a fraction of long-form.**
3. **The fastest money for a faceless-video skill is doing it for other people** while your
   own channel grows. Plan for that from week one.

---

## 1 · What a view is worth (India, 2026)

| Format | Typical RPM (₹ per 1,000 views) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form, India audience | **₹25 – ₹150** | Education/tech/money skew high; entertainment skews low |
| Long-form, mixed global audience | **₹80 – ₹300+** | A US/EU view can pay 10–40× an Indian one |
| Shorts, India audience | **₹5 – ₹30** | Reported ranges vary enormously; some datasets put Indian Shorts RPM near ₹1 |
| Shorts, US audience | **₹150 – ₹300** | This is why "make it for the world" is not just vanity |

Sources and ranges disagree because RPM depends on the viewer's country, the advertiser demand
that week, video length, mid-rolls, and season (Q4 is the peak). Treat all of these as
**planning ranges, not promises**. Published analyses also show Shorts RPM running at roughly
**3–14% of the same channel's long-form RPM** in almost every niche — so a Shorts view is
worth about a tenth of a long-form view, at best.

**The planning number to use:** ₹60 per 1,000 long-form views, ₹10 per 1,000 Shorts views.
If you beat that, great. If you budget higher and miss, you will quit in month three.

---

## 2 · The number you actually need: watch hours

Monetisation (before 1 Feb 2027) needs **4,000 public watch hours in 12 months** + 1,000 subs.

```
4,000 hours = 240,000 minutes

8-minute video, 50% average view duration  → 4 min per view → 60,000 views
8-minute video, 35% average view duration  → 2.8 min/view  → 86,000 views
12-minute video, 40% AVD                    → 4.8 min/view  → 50,000 views
```

So: **60,000 long-form views** is the realistic target, spread over however many videos. That
is roughly 20 videos doing 3,000 views each, or one video doing 60,000.

This is why Shorts alone cannot get you there — Shorts feed watch time does not count toward
the 4,000 hours. Only long-form (and Shorts watched on the watch page, which is rare).

## 3 · The honest 12-month revenue model

Assumptions: start 1 Nov 2026, 4 long-form + 18 Shorts per month, India-heavy audience at
₹60 long-form RPM, growing views. See `data/revenue-model.csv` for the raw numbers.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,200 | 6,000 | 80 | 40 | ₹0 |
| 2 | 2,500 | 20,000 | 250 | 120 | ₹0 |
| 3 | 5,000 | 60,000 | 580 | 260 | ₹0 |
| 4 | 9,000 | 120,000 | 1,180 | 430 | ₹0 |
| 5 | 14,000 | 220,000 | 2,110 | 640 | ₹0 |
| 6 | 20,000 | 350,000 | 3,440 | 900 | ₹0 – ₹1,200 (fan funding at 500 subs) |
| 7 | 30,000 | 500,000 | 5,440 | 1,250 | ₹1,800 (YPP month 1, partial) |
| 8 | 40,000 | 700,000 | 8,110 | 1,700 | ₹2,400 |
| 9 | 55,000 | 900,000 | 11,780 | 2,300 | ₹3,300 |
| 10 | 70,000 | 1,200,000 | 16,450 | 3,000 | ₹4,200 |

**Ads alone: ~₹2,700/month average over year one.** That is the truth nobody says out loud.
It is also why the channels that survive are the ones with a second and third income stream.

If you break into a global audience (English narration, neutral topics, US-heavy traffic),
multiply the ad column by 3–8×. That is the single biggest financial lever you have, and it
costs nothing: write for a global viewer, not a local one.

## 4 · Six income streams, ranked by how fast they pay

| # | Stream | When it starts | Realistic monthly (year 1) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Freelance stickman videos** (Fiverr/Upwork/local businesses) | Week 1 | ₹10,000 – ₹60,000 | Medium — you already have the pipeline |
| 2 | **Affiliates** (AI tools, TTS, editing software you actually use) | Week 1 | ₹500 – ₹15,000 | Low |
| 3 | **Digital products** (prompt packs, LUT/font/thumbnail packs, "stickman starter kit") | Month 2 | ₹1,000 – ₹20,000 | Medium once built |
| 4 | **Ads (YPP)** | Month 6–10 | ₹0 – ₹8,000 | Already doing it |
| 5 | **Sponsorships** (from ~10k subs; $50–$500 per integration for micro channels) | Month 8+ | ₹4,000 – ₹40,000 | Medium |
| 6 | **Memberships / Super Thanks / digital tips** | Fan-funding tier, 500 subs | ₹0 – ₹3,000 | Low |

Two structural notes:

- **Freelance work is not a distraction, it is the business.** A 6-minute stickman explainer
  sells for ₹3,000–₹15,000 depending on script and length. Three a month pays for every tool
  you will ever buy, and each job makes you faster at your own videos.
- **Affiliates need trust.** Only recommend tools you use on camera. One bad affiliate link can
  cost you more in credibility than it earns.

## 5 · Costs (so you know your real margin)

| Item | Free path | Paid path |
|---|---|---|
| Scripting/assistant | Free tiers | ₹1,700–₹3,500/mo |
| Voiceover | Gemini TTS / free tiers | ₹800–₹2,500/mo (ElevenLabs etc.) |
| Image/video generation | Free credits | ₹1,700–₹8,000/mo |
| Editing | CapCut / DaVinci Resolve free | ₹1,000–₹2,000/mo |
| Music/SFX | YouTube Audio Library (free) | ₹800–₹1,500/mo |
| Thumbnails | the pack in this repo + free Canva | ₹0 |
| **Total** | **₹0** | **₹5,000–₹17,000/mo** |

**Do not start on the paid path.** Start free, publish 10 videos, and only pay for the one
bottleneck that is actually slowing you down. Subscriptions are how new creators lose money
before they make any.

Break-even maths: at ₹60 RPM you need ~83,000 long-form views/month to cover ₹5,000 of tools.
One freelance client covers it with a single video.

## 6 · Taxes and payouts (India)

Not financial advice — confirm with a CA, but here is the shape of it:

- AdSense pays in **USD** to your Indian bank account. You will receive a **FIRC** (foreign
  inward remittance certificate) — keep every one.
- **TDS** is deducted at source on US-sourced earnings; the rate depends on the treaty forms
  you file. You can claim credit when filing your ITR.
- Ad revenue is **business/professional income**, not "other income". Track expenses (tools,
  hardware, internet) from day one — they reduce taxable profit.
- **GST** applies to export of services; above the threshold you must register, and filing an
  **LUT** avoids paying IGST on zero-rated exports. This is the single most common compliance
  mistake Indian creators make.
- Keep a simple ledger: date, platform, gross, fees, net, INR rate. Ten minutes a month.

## 7 · Milestones that matter more than money

| Milestone | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| 100 subs | You are not talking to an empty room |
| 500 subs + 3,000 hours | Fan funding tier: Super Thanks, memberships |
| 1,000 subs + 4,000 hours | **Full YPP** — ads. Apply the day you qualify |
| 10,000 subs | Sponsors start replying to your emails |
| 50,000 subs | Brand deals, licensing, real pricing power |
| 100,000 subs | You can hire an editor and become a studio |

Print this list. Cross things off. The gap between 0 and 1,000 is the hardest and the cheapest
part of the whole journey — everything after it compounds.

---

## 8 · The one-sentence business model

> Give away a genuinely useful, original stickman explanation every week; use Shorts to find
> the people who need it; sell your production skill to businesses while the audience grows;
> and let ad revenue become the pension, not the salary.
