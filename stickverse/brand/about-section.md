# StickVerse — channel description (About section)

Paste-ready. The **first line matters most**: YouTube shows it in search results and in the
channel preview, so it has to work on its own with no context.

Pick **A**, paste, done. B and C are alternates if you want a different tone.

---

## A · Recommended (246 characters)

```text
Stickman stories that explain you.

Psychology, money and true stories, turned into short hand-drawn films. No faces, no filler — just a story and why it matters.

New Shorts daily · Deep dives every Friday.

Collabs: stickverse.contact@gmail.com
```

**Why this one:** line 1 is a promise a stranger understands in two seconds, line 2 says what
they will actually get, line 3 sets expectations, line 4 makes you look reachable. Nothing is
padded and nothing is hype.

## Rules for this section

- **First line does the work.** Assume nobody reads past it.
- **No emoji walls, no "welcome to my channel", no "subscribe for more".** Those read as
  decoration; the About is a promise, not a greeting.
- **Links belong in the Links section**, not the description — they stay clickable and the
  text stays readable.
- **Keep the schedule line honest.** If you post three Shorts a week, write three. A broken
  schedule line costs more trust than a slower one.
- **Hashtags go on videos**, not in the About.
- **Update it when the channel changes shape.** The About should describe video 30, not video 1.
